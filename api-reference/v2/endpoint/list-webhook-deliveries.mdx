---
title: "List Deliveries"
description: "List webhook delivery attempts for your workspace."
openapi: "api-reference/v2/openapi.json GET /webhooks/indexing/deliveries"
---

Returns the delivery history for your workspace, most recent first. Use it to investigate events that never arrived, or that arrived more than once.

Filter by `status` to isolate failures. It takes one of these states:

- `pending`: recorded and waiting to be sent.
- `sweeping`: claimed for delivery or retry.
- `delivered`: your endpoint returned a `2xx`.
- `failed`: an attempt failed and will be retried.
- `permanently_failed`: retries are exhausted. No further attempts.

`limit` is 1 to 100 (default 20). To get the next page, pass the response's `next_cursor` as `cursor`. `next_cursor` is `null` on the last page. A `limit` outside that range returns `422`, and an unknown `status` returns `400`.

See [Webhooks](/essentials/v2/webhooks) for retry behavior.
