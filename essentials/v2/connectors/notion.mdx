The Notion connector reads the databases and top-level pages you share with its integration. Each page becomes one document: a database row, a selected page, and every sub-page under them.

Each sub-page is linked to the page it sits under, so a page tree stays connected.

## Before you connect

Notion uses an internal integration token.

**Credentials**

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string" required>
A Notion internal integration token. It starts with `secret_` or `ntn_`.
</ResponseField>

**Capabilities to give the integration**

- `Read content`: required. Lets HydraDB search, read pages and blocks, and query databases.
- `Read user information without email`: optional. Lets HydraDB check the token and show who created a page.

**Set it up**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an integration">
    In Notion, create an internal integration with the Read content capability, and copy its token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share content with it">
    Share each database and page you want synced with the integration. Discovery lists only what the integration can see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the token as `access_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select resources">
    Discover lists every database the integration can see, and every page that sits at the top of the workspace. Select at least one: a sync needs a selected resource.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use provider `notion` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list what the credentials can reach, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

Create one connector per account, each with that account's own credentials.

To connect several Notion accounts, give each connector its own `provider_account_scope`, for example `notion-account-a` and `notion-account-b`. Both are caller-provided example identifiers. HydraDB stores the value as given and does not check it against Notion's own account ids. Two connectors that share a scope and write to the same collection can overwrite each other's items. See [several accounts from one provider](/essentials/v2/connectors#6-connect-several-accounts-from-one-provider).

## What gets synced

**What the labels mean**

- **Title:** names the item in results.
- **Searchable:** part of the text a query matches.
- **Date:** the time HydraDB uses for recency.
- **Filter:** a key a query can filter on, typed exactly as shown.
- **Link:** connects this item to another item.
- **Person or group in the graph:** a person or group the knowledge graph attaches the item to.
- **Access:** decides who can read the item.
- **Stored with results:** kept on the document, and shown when you open or inspect it. A search does not match it.
- **Not kept:** the provider has it, and HydraDB does not keep it.

### Pages

- **One document:** One Notion page: a database row, a selected page, or any sub-page under them. A sub-page is its own document, not part of its parent's text.
- **Title:** The page's title. A page without a title uses its Notion id.
- **Text:** The page title at the top, the creator as an `Author` line, and the plain text of the page's blocks in order, one block per line: paragraphs, headings, bulleted and numbered lists, to-dos, toggles, quotes, callouts and code. Text inside toggles and callouts is included.
- **Date:** When the page was last edited.
- **Link back:** The page in Notion.
- **Files:** Images and files are kept as links to the original. Text in images can be added when HydraDB's image reading is available. The text of PDF and Word files is not guaranteed to be read.

## How it fits into the graph

**What each item becomes**

- Pages: a page, a page, article or other document-style content.

**Connections Notion creates**

- A sub-page to the page it sits under.
- No links from Notion pages to other tools. In the other direction, GitHub issues, pull requests and comments that link to a Notion page are linked to it.

**People and groups it names**

- The page's creator, with an email when Notion returns one.
- People @mentioned in the page.

**What HydraDB reads from the text**

Everything in the page text: topics, and people or projects named in plain text rather than @mentioned. Database properties are not read, so structure kept only in database columns is not available.

There is no per-connector setup for this. Every connector maps its items onto the same shared model, and HydraDB finds people, organizations, projects, products and other entities in the text the same way for every source. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Permissions

<Warning>
This connector does not carry Notion permissions into search. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

- Notion's page sharing is not read.

**Enforce ACL** cannot be turned on for Notion, because it needs a provider that reports `acl_supported: true`. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

Database and collection names decide where content lands; collection names do not authorize access.

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** A database reaches back 30 days by last edited time by default, set by `lookback_days`. A selected page and its sub-pages are walked on every sync.
- **New and changed items:** For a database, rows edited since the last sync are read again in full, with their sub-pages. For a selected page, the whole tree is walked on every sync and pages edited since the last sync are sent.
- **Edits:** An edited page is sent again and replaces its document. A sub-page under a database row is picked up only when the row itself is edited.
- **Deletions:** Pages deleted or unshared in Notion are not removed. They stay searchable in HydraDB.
- Sub-pages are followed up to 10 levels deep, and blocks up to 20 levels deep.
- A sub-page or block that Notion now refuses or cannot find is skipped instead of failing its page.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` to change it.

## Not synced

- Database property values other than the title.
- Comments on pages.
- Pages and databases not shared with the integration.
- Text in unsupported blocks, such as table rows, equations, embeds and bookmarks.
- Deletions: deleted pages stay in HydraDB.

## Questions this lets you answer

- What does our onboarding page say about laptop setup?
- Which Notion pages did Priya create?
- Which pages mention Alex?
- What sub-pages sit under the Engineering Handbook page?

## Good to know

- **Database columns are not synced.** A database row brings in its title and page text only. Status, owner, dates and tags are not read.
- **Edits to a sub-page under a database row can be missed.** They are picked up when the row itself is edited. Pages you select directly do not have this problem, because they are walked on every sync.
- **Only the last 30 days of a database come in first.** Set `lookback_days` to reach further back.
