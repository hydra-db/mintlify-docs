Sync customers, invoices, subscriptions and charges from one Stripe account. Each record is one document with a readable summary, and an invoice carries its line items. Invoices, subscriptions and charges link to their customer.

Stripe permissions are not imported, so every synced record is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Other Stripe objects, such as products, refunds and payouts, are not synced. Deletions in Stripe are not detected.

<Warning>
Searchable amounts assume two decimal places. For a currency such as JPY, an amount of 100 is shown as 1.00, so the searchable text understates it by a factor of 100. Original stored amounts are unchanged.
</Warning>

## Permissions

<Warning>
Stripe permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Stripe uses a secret key together with the account id.

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
A Stripe secret key, `sk_live_...` or `sk_test_...`, with read access to the account's resources.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_id" type="string" required>
The Stripe account id, `acct_...`.
</ResponseField>

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the secret key as `client_secret` and the account id as `account_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account">
    Select the account's `All data` resource. It syncs customers, invoices, subscriptions and charges together. You cannot select these record types separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one Stripe account. A test-mode key reads test data and a live-mode key reads live data.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `stripe` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync lists records created in the sync window, and reads Stripe's events for updates in the same window.
- **Edits:** An update, such as an invoice being paid, arrives as an event and replaces the record's document.
- **Deletions:** Deletions in Stripe are not detected. A deleted record stays in HydraDB.
- Updates are read from events that reach back 30 days. After a long pause, updates to older records can be missed.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Stripe item

<ResponseField name="created" type="Result date">
When the record was created. It stays the result date after the record is updated, so ranking by date follows creation time. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.stripe_id" type="Query filter">
The Stripe id: `cus_...`, `in_...`, `sub_...` or `ch_...`. Targets one record. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `stripe`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type: `customers`, `invoices`, `subscriptions` or `charges`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other Stripe fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field Stripe returns for the record, such as a customer's address, metadata or invoice settings. Kept for inspection only. A query does not match them and cannot filter on them.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Customers

- **One document:** One customer.
- **Title:** The customer's name, or their email when there is no name.
- **Text:** The customer's description, then `Name`, `Email`, `Phone` and `Balance` lines, with the balance in its currency. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the customer was created.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="email" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's email. It identifies the customer as a person in the graph, appears in the `Email` line, and is the title when there is no name.
</ResponseField>

### Invoices

- **One document:** One invoice, with its line items.
- **Title:** `Invoice <number> - <customer>`, using the customer's name or email.
- **Text:** The invoice description, then `Customer`, `Email`, `Status`, `Total`, `Amount due`, `Amount paid`, `Due date` and `Line items` lines. Each line item shows its description and amount. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the invoice was created.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Invoice PDFs are not downloaded.

<ResponseField name="Line items" type="Searchable text">
Each line's description and amount, written into the `Line items` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Graph connection">
The customer id. Links the invoice to its customer. The id itself is not searchable. The customer's name or email is part of the text.
</ResponseField>

### Subscriptions

- **One document:** One subscription.
- **Title:** The plan's nickname or name, or the plan id, followed by `subscription`.
- **Text:** `Plan`, `Status`, `Amount`, `Quantity`, `Customer`, `Started` and `Current period end` lines. `Amount` is the plan price with its currency and interval, for example `49.00 USD/month`. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the subscription was created.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="plan" type="Searchable text">
The plan nickname or name, or the plan id when neither is available, forms the title followed by `subscription`. Its price and billing interval form the searchable `Amount` line. Product or price details that live only in other fields are not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
The customer id links the subscription to its customer. The searchable `Customer` line uses the customer's name when known, or the id otherwise.
</ResponseField>

### Charges

- **One document:** One charge.
- **Title:** The charge description, or `Charge <amount currency> - <customer>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The charge description, then `Customer`, `Amount`, `Status`, `Receipt email` and `Failure message` lines. `Customer` is the first of the billing name, billing email, receipt email, customer name and customer id that is present. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the charge was created.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Graph connection">
The customer id. Links the charge to its customer. The id itself is not searchable unless it is the only customer detail, in which case it fills the `Customer` line.
</ResponseField>

**Example searches**

- What status, amount due and amount paid are recorded for Acme Corp's invoice INV-0042?
- What plan is jane@example.com subscribed to, and when does the current period end?
- Why did the charge for jane@example.com fail?

## How it fits into the graph

- Customers use the Record class.
- Invoices use the Record class.
- Subscriptions use the Record class.
- Charges use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Stripe creates**

- An invoice to its customer.
- A subscription to its customer.
- A charge to its customer.
- Charges are not linked to their invoice, and invoices are not linked to their subscription.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- Each customer, identified by their email.

**Found in the text**

Descriptions, plan names and the rest of each record's summary. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Products, prices and plans as their own documents.
- Events, payouts, refunds, disputes, balance transactions and other Stripe objects.
- Deletions in Stripe.
- Invoice PDFs and hosted invoice pages.
