Sync account structure and reports from every Google Ads account one Google user can access: accounts and labels, campaigns with their budgets, targeting criteria and labels, ad groups, ads, audiences, keywords, placements, search terms, geography, video, and daily campaign and ad group performance. Each report row is one document, written as `Label: value` lines.

Google Ads account access is not imported, so every synced row is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Rows are kept by each report's key, and some keys leave out the date or the account, so several rows can end up as one document. Items removed in Google Ads stay in HydraDB.

## Permissions

<Warning>
Google Ads permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Google Ads uses a developer token together with a Google OAuth client id, client secret and refresh token. Paste all four.

<ResponseField name="developer_token" type="secret string" required>
Your Google Ads API developer token.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="oauth_credentials.client_id" type="secret string" required>
A Google OAuth client id.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="oauth_credentials.client_secret" type="secret string" required>
The secret for that OAuth client.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="oauth_credentials.refresh_token" type="secret string" required>
A refresh token for a Google user who has access to the Google Ads accounts you want to sync.
</ResponseField>

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather the credentials">
    Get your Google Ads API developer token. Then issue a refresh token, through your own Google OAuth client, for a Google user who can access the Google Ads accounts to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the developer token as `developer_token`, and the client id, client secret and refresh token as `oauth_credentials.client_id`, `oauth_credentials.client_secret` and `oauth_credentials.refresh_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account-wide resource">
    HydraDB lists one resource that covers every account the user can access. Select it. Individual Google Ads accounts are not listed separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads every Google Ads account that one Google user can access, including accounts under a manager account.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `googleads` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Campaign, ad group, budget, demographic, location and geography performance reports are read for the days in each sync's date window.
- **Edits:** A row read again updates its document only while the row's report key stays the same. In reports whose key includes a name or status, such as campaign performance, renaming a campaign or changing its status creates a new document and the old one stays. Corrections Google makes later to older days are not guaranteed to be picked up.
- **Deletions:** Campaigns, ad groups and ads removed in Google Ads stay searchable in HydraDB.
- Manager accounts and disabled accounts appear in the account hierarchy, but their performance and other reports are not read.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### Report rows

- **One document:** One row of a report, identified by that report's key. Rows that share a key are kept as one document, and the last one read wins. Budget rows for different days share a key, managed placement rows share one key per account and date, and campaign performance rows with the same campaign name, status, date and device share a key even when they come from different accounts.
- **Title:** The row's most specific name: its own name or title, else the ad group, budget, audience, label, interest, geo target, video title, campaign or account name, else any other name column. When the row has a `segments__date`, the date follows in parentheses, as in `Spring Sale (2026-11-03)`. A row with no name is titled `Google Ads <report>`.
- **Text:** The title on the first line. Then every non-empty column of the row in alphabetical order, as `Label: value`, with the column name turned into words, such as `Metrics Cost Micros`. Nested values are flattened first. Ids and resource names are left out. Rows of `accessible_customers` add a `Resource names` line.
- **Date:** The report date, at midnight UTC. A row without a date has no date.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="Report columns" type="Searchable text">
Names, statuses, dates and metric values, one `Label: value` line each. No unit conversion is applied: columns such as `cost_micros` and `amount_micros` keep the raw micros values Google Ads reports. Ids and resource names are not part of the text.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="segments__date" type="Searchable text, Result date">
The report day, from `segments__date`, or from `date` when that is the column a report has. It sets the result date and appears as a column line. `segments__date` is also added to the title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.googleads_stream" type="Query filter">
The report the row came from. Limits a query to one report. The values are `accessible_customers`, `customer_hierarchy`, `customer`, `customer_label`, `label`, `campaign_history`, `campaign_budget`, `campaign_criterion`, `campaign_label`, `campaign_performance`, `campaign_performance_by_age_range_and_device`, `campaign_performance_by_gender_and_device`, `campaign_performance_by_location`, `adgroups`, `ad_groups_performance`, `ad_group_ad`, `ad_group_ad_label`, `ad_group_criterion`, `ad_group_label`, `ad_listing_group_criterion`, `audience`, `user_interest`, `geo_performance`, `geo_target_constant`, `keyword_view`, `managed_placement_view`, `search_term_view` and `video`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's rows. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `googleads`. Limits a query to Google Ads rows. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The report the row came from, shown when you inspect the item. To limit a query to one report, use `additional_metadata.googleads_stream`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other report columns" type="Stored only">
Every column of the row as Google Ads returned it, including ids and resource names. Kept for inspection only. A query does not match ids or resource names and cannot filter on these columns.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- How did the Spring Sale campaign perform on mobile on 2026-11-03?
- What amount in micros is recorded for the budget named Brand Budget?
- Which keywords are in the Running Shoes ad group, and how did they perform?

## How it fits into the graph

- Report rows use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Google Ads creates**

- None. Rows are not linked to their account, campaign, ad group or ad.
- Campaign labels are not linked to their campaign. A campaign label row carries the campaign id and the label name, but no campaign name.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

No people or groups are attached to rows.

**Found in the text**

The row text: names, statuses, dates and metric values. Ids and resource names are not part of it. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Per-click reports.
- A standalone gender report. Campaign performance by gender and device is synced.
- Performance and other reports for manager accounts and disabled accounts.
- Removals: campaigns, ad groups and ads removed in Google Ads stay in HydraDB.
- Google Ads account access.
