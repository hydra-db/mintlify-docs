## What is HydraDB? 

If you're building agents that need context from workspace or personal apps, need to remember user preferences, need to take actions using users' context, or will be dealing with an ever-growing knowledge base, HydraDB is for you.

HydraDB is <u>The Brain</u> behind AI for all your working context: 

- **User memories.** Preferences, history, and identity.
- **Semantic knowledge.** Documents, app sources, and facts.
- **Episodic experiences.** Time-ordered events from interactions.

---

## The problem

> _GraphDBs don't store context. VectorDBs find what's similar._ 
>
> _Your agents want what's useful._

GraphDBs cannot store full context of documents. Vector search can be meaning-blind. It finds the closest matching embeddings to your query and stops there. It can't tell "Python" the programming language from "Python" the snake. 

Ultimately, your AI has no answers for "who owns this customer escalation." or "how has this projected evolved over the last 3 years?" or your agents serve identical results to everyone. Your AE searching "project Acme" needs the latest sales deck and competitive notes but an engineer running the same query needs the changelog and architecture decisions.

Most teams building stateful AI end up stitching together five systems: a vector database, a graph database, a relational store, filesystem primitives, and a \[temporal store\]. You spend months planning, write brittle code, and maintain it constantly. Everything still fails in production. Either embeddings miscalculate similarity, results aren't relevant or personalized, or costs scale exponentially. Ultimately, your agents and evals suffer.

HydraDB solves that by giving you one graph that learns every user preference, retains every agent experience, and delivers personalized context the moment your agent needs it. We help you build intelligent, stateful AI.
