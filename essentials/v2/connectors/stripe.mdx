The Stripe connector reads one Stripe account. Customers, invoices, subscriptions and charges each become one document with a readable summary, and an invoice carries its line items.

Invoices, subscriptions and charges are linked to the customer they belong to.

## Before you connect

Stripe uses a secret key together with the account id.

**Credentials**

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
A Stripe secret key, `sk_live_...` or `sk_test_...`, with read access to the account's resources.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_id" type="string" required>
The Stripe account id, `acct_...`.
</ResponseField>

**Set it up**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the secret key as `client_secret` and the account id as `account_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account">
    Discover offers one `All data` resource for the account. There are no smaller parts to pick. Select it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use provider `stripe` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list what the credentials can reach, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

Create one connector per account, each with that account's own credentials. One connector reads one Stripe account. A test-mode key reads test data and a live-mode key reads live data.

## What gets synced

<Warning>
Amounts in the text are shown in major units, assuming two decimal places. For a zero-decimal currency, the amounts read 100 times too small. The stored numbers are unchanged.
</Warning>

**What the labels mean**

- **Title:** names the item in results.
- **Searchable:** part of the text a query matches.
- **Date:** the time HydraDB uses for recency.
- **Filter:** a key a query can filter on, typed exactly as shown.
- **Link:** connects this item to another item.
- **Person or group in the graph:** a person or group the knowledge graph attaches the item to.
- **Access:** decides who can read the item.
- **Stored with results:** kept on the document, and shown when you open or inspect it. A search does not match it.
- **Not kept:** the provider has it, and HydraDB does not keep it.

### On every Stripe document

**Date**

<ResponseField name="created" type="Date">

</ResponseField>

**Filter**

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.stripe_id" type="Filter">
The Stripe id: `cus_...`, `in_...`, `sub_...` or `ch_...`. Targets one record. A customer's id is also what its invoices, subscriptions and charges link to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Filter">
Limits a query to this connector.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Filter">
Always `stripe`.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored with results (2)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored with results">
The record type: `customers`, `invoices`, `subscriptions` or `charges`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other Stripe fields" type="Stored with results">
Every other field Stripe returns for the record, such as an address, metadata or invoice settings. A search does not match them, and they are not filters.
</ResponseField>

### Customers

- **One document:** One customer.
- **Title:** The customer's name, or their email when there is no name.
- **Text:** The customer's description, then `Name`, `Email`, `Phone` and `Balance` lines, with the balance in its currency.
- **Date:** When the customer was created.
- **Link back:** None.

**Title**

<ResponseField name="title" type="Title, Searchable">
Names the customer in results.
</ResponseField>

**Searchable**

<ResponseField name="Customer text" type="Searchable">
The description, name, email, phone and balance, as described above.
</ResponseField>

**Person or group in the graph**

<ResponseField name="email" type="Person or group in the graph, Searchable, Title">
The customer's email. It identifies the customer as a person in the graph, appears in the `Email` line, and is the title when there is no name.
</ResponseField>

### Invoices

- **One document:** One invoice, with its line items.
- **Title:** `Invoice <number> - <customer>`, using the customer's name or email.
- **Text:** The invoice description, then `Customer`, `Email`, `Status`, `Total`, `Amount due`, `Amount paid`, `Due date` and `Line items` lines. Each line item shows its description and amount.
- **Date:** When the invoice was created.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Invoice PDFs are not downloaded.

**Title**

<ResponseField name="title" type="Title, Searchable">
Names the invoice in results.
</ResponseField>

**Searchable**

<ResponseField name="Invoice text" type="Searchable">
The description, customer, status, amounts, due date and line items, as described above.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Line items" type="Searchable">
Each line's description and amount, written into the `Line items` line.
</ResponseField>

**Link**

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Link">
The customer id. Links the invoice to its customer's document.
</ResponseField>

### Subscriptions

- **One document:** One subscription.
- **Title:** The plan's nickname or name, or the plan id, followed by `subscription`.
- **Text:** `Plan`, `Status`, `Amount`, `Quantity`, `Customer`, `Started` and `Current period end` lines. `Amount` is the plan price with its currency and interval, for example `49.00 USD/month`. `Customer` is the customer's name, or the id when the name is not known.
- **Date:** When the subscription was created.
- **Link back:** None.

**Title**

<ResponseField name="plan" type="Title, Searchable">
The subscription's plan. Its nickname or name is the title, and its price and interval form the `Amount` line.
</ResponseField>

**Searchable**

<ResponseField name="Subscription text" type="Searchable">
The plan, status, price, quantity, customer and period dates, as described above.
</ResponseField>

**Link**

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Link, Searchable">
The customer id. Links the subscription to its customer's document. The customer's name appears in the text.
</ResponseField>

### Charges

- **One document:** One charge.
- **Title:** The charge description, or `Charge <amount currency> - <customer>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The charge description, then `Customer`, `Amount`, `Status`, `Receipt email` and `Failure message` lines. `Customer` is the first of the billing name, billing email, receipt email, customer name and customer id that is present.
- **Date:** When the charge was created.
- **Link back:** None.

**Title**

<ResponseField name="title" type="Title, Searchable">
Names the charge in results.
</ResponseField>

**Searchable**

<ResponseField name="Charge text" type="Searchable">
The description, payer, amount, status and failure reason, as described above.
</ResponseField>

**Link**

<ResponseField name="customer" type="Link">
The customer id. Links the charge to its customer's document.
</ResponseField>

## How it fits into the graph

**What each item becomes**

- Customers: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.
- Invoices: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.
- Subscriptions: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.
- Charges: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.

**Connections Stripe creates**

- An invoice to its customer.
- A subscription to its customer.
- A charge to its customer.
- Charges are not linked to their invoice, and invoices are not linked to their subscription.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups it names**

- Each customer, identified by their email.

**What HydraDB reads from the text**

Products and plans as entities, and anything written in descriptions.

There is no per-connector setup for this. Every connector maps its items onto the same shared model, and HydraDB finds people, organizations, projects, products and other entities in the text the same way for every source. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Permissions

<Warning>
This connector does not carry Stripe permissions into search. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

**Enforce ACL** cannot be turned on for Stripe, because it needs a provider that reports `acl_supported: true`. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

Database and collection names decide where content lands; collection names do not authorize access.

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync lists records created in the sync window, and reads Stripe's events for updates in the same window.
- **Edits:** An update, such as an invoice being paid, arrives as an event and replaces the record's document.
- **Deletions:** Deletions in Stripe are not detected. A deleted record stays in HydraDB.
- Updates are read from events that reach back 30 days. After a long pause, updates to older records can be missed.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` to change it.

## Not synced

- Products, prices and plans as their own documents.
- Events, payouts, refunds, disputes, balance transactions and other Stripe objects.
- Deletions in Stripe.
- Invoice PDFs and hosted invoice pages.

## Questions this lets you answer

- What status, amount due and amount paid are recorded for Acme Corp's invoice INV-0042?
- What plan is jane@example.com subscribed to, and when does the current period end?
- Why did the charge for jane@example.com fail?
- What line items were on Acme Corp's last invoice?

## Good to know

- **Recency follows creation time.** A record's date stays its creation time after it is updated, so recency ranks records by when they were created.
- **Plan details come from the plan only.** Product or price details that live only in other fields are not searchable.
