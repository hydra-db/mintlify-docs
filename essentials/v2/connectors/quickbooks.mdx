Sync customers, invoices, payments, bills and attachments from one QuickBooks Online company. Each record is one document. Invoices and payments link to their customer. Each attachment is its own document, linked to the records it is attached to, and its file is read so its text is searchable.

QuickBooks permissions are not imported, so every synced record is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Every sync reads all records of each selected type again, which is slow for companies with many invoices. Records deleted in QuickBooks stay in HydraDB. Vendors, items and accounts are not their own documents.

<Note>
QuickBooks is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

<Warning>
QuickBooks permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

- Each record type you select is its own resource, so you can set an access rule on one type, such as Invoices, and not on the others.

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

QuickBooks uses the client id and client secret of an Intuit developer app, with a refresh token and the company id of the company you connect.

<ResponseField name="client_id" type="secret string" required>
The client id of your Intuit developer app, from its Keys & credentials page.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
The client secret of the same app.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="refresh_token" type="secret string" required>
An OAuth refresh token for the company.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="realmId" type="string" required>
The QuickBooks company id that the refresh token belongs to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string">
Leave it empty. HydraDB obtains access tokens itself.
</ResponseField>

Intuit replaces the refresh token from time to time. HydraDB stores each new refresh token itself.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Intuit app">
    In the Intuit developer portal, create an app and copy its client id and client secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a refresh token and company id">
    Get a refresh token for the company you want to connect, and that company's id. The Intuit OAuth Playground is one place to get both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the values as `client_id`, `client_secret`, `refresh_token` and `realmId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select record types">
    HydraDB lists five record types: Customers, Invoices, Payments, Bills and Attachments. Select the ones to sync. Each selected type is synced on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one QuickBooks Online company.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `quickbooks` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reads every record of each selected type, whatever its date. `lookback_days` does not narrow it.
- **New and changed items:** Every sync reads every record of each selected type again.
- **Edits:** A changed record replaces its document.
- **Deletions:** Deleted records are not removed. A record deleted in QuickBooks stays searchable in HydraDB.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every QuickBooks item

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the record was last updated in QuickBooks. It is the result date, and it appears in the text.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the record was created in QuickBooks. It is the result date only when there is no update time. Searchable on customers. Not searchable on other record types.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.quickbooks_id" type="Query filter">
The record's QuickBooks Id, without its record type. Finds a record by its Id. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `quickbooks`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type: `Customer`, `Invoice`, `Payment`, `Bill` or `Attachable`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other QuickBooks fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field QuickBooks returns for the record. Kept for inspection only. A query does not match them and cannot filter on them.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Customers

- **One document:** One customer, active or inactive.
- **Title:** The customer's display name. Otherwise the fully qualified name, the company name, or the first and last name.
- **Text:** Contact details: the display name, first and last name, email, phone numbers, website, whether the customer is a person or an organization, the company or parent customer name, and the created and updated times. Then the customer's QuickBooks notes, followed by `Name`, `Company`, `Email`, `Phone` and `Balance` lines. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the customer was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Files attached to a customer arrive as separate attachment documents linked to it.

<ResponseField name="display_name" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's display name. It forms the title and names the customer in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="first_name" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's given name. Helps form the person's name when there is no display name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="last_name" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's family name. Helps form the person's name when there is no display name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="email" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's primary email. Identifies a person customer in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="entity_type" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
Organization when the customer has a company name, otherwise person. Decides whether the graph records the customer as a person or as an organization.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider_user_id" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The customer's QuickBooks Id, for person customers only. Empty for organizations. Identifies the person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="phones" type="Searchable text">
The primary, mobile, alternate and fax numbers, each once. Phone numbers do not identify the customer in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="url" type="Searchable text">
The customer's website. It is part of the text, not a link back to QuickBooks.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="organization_name" type="Searchable text">
The company name for an organization, or the parent customer's name for a person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="organization_id" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
For a person, the parent customer's id. Links the person to that parent customer. Empty for organizations.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="emails" type="Stored only">
An empty list. Secondary email addresses are not kept.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Invoices

- **One document:** One invoice.
- **Title:** `Invoice <number> - <customer>`.
- **Text:** The customer memo, then `Customer`, `Total`, `Balance`, `Invoice date`, `Due date`, `Line items` and `Note` lines, and the update time. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the invoice was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Files attached to an invoice arrive as separate attachment documents linked to it.

<ResponseField name="CustomerRef" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
The customer the invoice belongs to. Its id links the invoice to the customer's document. Its name is in the title and the `Customer` line. The id itself is not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="DocNumber" type="Searchable text">
The invoice number, in the title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Line items" type="Searchable text">
Each line's description with its item or account name, written into the `Line items` line. Other line details are not searchable.
</ResponseField>

### Payments

- **One document:** One customer payment.
- **Title:** `Payment <reference> - <customer>`.
- **Text:** The private note, then `Customer`, `Total`, `Payment date` and `Reference` lines, and the update time. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the payment was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Files attached to a payment arrive as separate attachment documents linked to it.

<ResponseField name="CustomerRef" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
The customer who paid. Its id links the payment to the customer's document. Its name is in the title and the `Customer` line. The id itself is not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="PaymentRefNum" type="Searchable text">
The payment reference, in the title and the `Reference` line.
</ResponseField>

### Bills

- **One document:** One vendor bill.
- **Title:** `Bill <number> - <vendor>`.
- **Text:** The private note, then `Vendor`, `Total`, `Balance`, `Bill date`, `Due date` and `Line items` lines, and the update time. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the bill was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Files attached to a bill arrive as separate attachment documents linked to it.

<ResponseField name="VendorRef" type="Searchable text">
The vendor's name, in the title and the `Vendor` line. Vendors are not their own documents, so a bill is not linked to a vendor.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="DocNumber" type="Searchable text">
The bill number, in the title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Line items" type="Searchable text">
Each line's description with its item or account name, written into the `Line items` line. Other line details are not searchable.
</ResponseField>

### Attachments

- **One document:** One attachment: a file or a note.
- **Title:** The file name.
- **Text:** When the file's text is read, that text is the document's text. Otherwise, the attachment note, then `File name`, `Content type` and `File size` lines. The title and the update time are searchable either way.
- **Date:** When the attachment was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** The file is downloaded and its text is read. A file larger than 50 MiB is not downloaded, and only its note and file details are searchable.

<ResponseField name="File text" type="Searchable text">
The text read from the file, such as a PDF attached to an invoice.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Note" type="Searchable text">
The attachment note. Searchable only when the file's text was not read. Read file text takes its place.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ContentType, Size" type="Searchable text">
The file's media type and size. Searchable only when the file's text was not read. Otherwise kept for inspection.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="AttachableRef" type="Graph connection">
Every QuickBooks record the file is attached to. Links the attachment to each of those records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="parent_id" type="Graph connection">
The first record the file is attached to. The graph places the attachment under that record. Not searchable, and not a query filter.
</ResponseField>

**Example searches**

- What is the outstanding balance on invoices for Acme Corp?
- What does the contract PDF attached to invoice 1042 say?
- Which bills from Office Depot still have a balance?

## How it fits into the graph

- Customers use the Contact class.
- Invoices use the Page class.
- Payments use the Page class.
- Bills use the Page class.
- Attachments use the Page class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections QuickBooks creates**

- An invoice to its customer.
- A payment to its customer.
- An attachment to every record it is attached to, and under the first of them.
- A person customer to its parent customer, when it has one.
- Bills are not linked to a vendor.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- Each customer: as a person, or as an organization when it has a company name.
- For a person customer with a parent customer, the parent as their organization.
- Vendors are not attached as people or organizations. They appear in bill text only.

**Found in the text**

Customer notes, invoice memos, payment and bill notes, line items and attachment text. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Record types you did not select.
- Vendors, items, accounts, estimates, sales receipts, credit memos, journal entries and other QuickBooks record types. Vendors, items and accounts appear only as names in bills and line items.
- QuickBooks reports, such as profit and loss or the balance sheet.
- Deletions: records deleted in QuickBooks stay in HydraDB.
- The text of files larger than 50 MiB.
