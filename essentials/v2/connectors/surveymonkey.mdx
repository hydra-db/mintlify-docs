Sync the SurveyMonkey surveys you select. Each survey is one document with its page text and question wording. Each response is one document of question and answer pairs, linked to its survey. A respondent who gave an email is attached to the response as a person.

SurveyMonkey team sharing is not imported, so every synced survey and response is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. On SurveyMonkey Basic plans, only 25 responses per survey come through. Surveys and responses deleted in SurveyMonkey stay in HydraDB.

## Permissions

<Warning>
SurveyMonkey permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

- Every synced survey and response carries a fixed `additional_metadata.rbac_warning` note saying SurveyMonkey sharing is not captured. The note does not decide who can read the item. An access rule you set still applies.

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

SurveyMonkey uses an app access token.

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string" required>
The access token of a SurveyMonkey app with the scopes below.
</ResponseField>

**Scopes the token needs**

- `surveys_read`: View Surveys, to list surveys and read their pages and questions.
- `responses_read_detail`: View Response Details, to read responses with readable answers.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a SurveyMonkey app">
    Create an app in SurveyMonkey and give its token View Surveys and View Response Details. A draft app's token expires after 90 days unless you deploy the app. Replace the token in the connector before then.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the app's access token as `access_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select surveys">
    HydraDB lists the surveys the token can see, by title. Only the first 100 surveys are listed. Select the surveys to sync. Each one syncs separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads the surveys one SurveyMonkey token can see.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `surveymonkey` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default, by when each survey and response was last modified, or created if there is no modification time.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads the questionnaires and responses of each selected survey that were modified in its window.
- **Edits:** A modified questionnaire or response replaces its document. A questionnaire last modified before the window is not read, even while its new responses sync.
- **Deletions:** Deleted surveys and responses are not removed. A survey or response deleted in SurveyMonkey stays searchable in HydraDB.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every SurveyMonkey item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.surveymonkey_id" type="Query filter">
The SurveyMonkey id of the survey or response. Targets one item. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.rbac_warning" type="Query filter">
A fixed note that SurveyMonkey sharing is not captured. It does not decide who can read the item. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's items. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `surveymonkey`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type, `survey_details` or `simplified_responses`, shown when you inspect the item.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Surveys

- **One document:** One selected survey's questionnaire.
- **Title:** The survey title, else its nickname, else `SurveyMonkey survey <id>`.
- **Text:** The nickname when it differs from the title, then each page's title and description and every question, one per line, in survey order. Answer choices are not part of the text. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the survey was last modified, or created if there is no modification time.
- **Link back:** The survey's preview page, else its analyze page, else its address in SurveyMonkey's API.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="nickname" type="Searchable text">
The survey's alternate name. It is the title when there is no survey title, and is written into the text when it differs from the title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Pages and questions" type="Searchable text">
Each page's title and description and each question's wording, in the order the survey shows them.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="date_modified, date_created" type="Result date">
When the survey was last modified and created. The modification time, else the creation time, sets the result date. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="Answer choices" type="Stored only">
The answer choices each question offers, when SurveyMonkey supplies them. Kept for inspection only. A choice a respondent selected is searchable on their response.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other survey fields" type="Stored only">
Other survey fields SurveyMonkey returns. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Responses

- **One document:** One response to a selected survey.
- **Title:** `SurveyMonkey response <response id>`. The title does not name the survey.
- **Text:** The title, then `Respondent` and `Email` lines, then a `Q:` line with the question and an `A:` line with the answer for each answered question. Several answers to one question are joined with `;`. Unanswered questions are left out.
- **Date:** When the response was last modified, or created if there is no modification time.
- **Link back:** The response's analyze page, else its address in SurveyMonkey's API.
- **Files:** None. Only answer text is kept.

<ResponseField name="email_address" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The respondent's email, when the survey collected it, written as the `Email` line. It attaches the respondent to the response as a person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="first_name, last_name" type="Searchable text">
The respondent's name, when collected, written as the `Respondent` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Questions and answers" type="Searchable text">
Each answered question's wording and the readable answer text.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="survey_id" type="Graph connection">
The survey the response belongs to. Links the response to its survey. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.surveymonkey_survey_id" type="Query filter">
The survey id. Limits a query to the responses of one survey. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="date_modified, date_created" type="Result date">
When the response was last modified and created. The modification time, else the creation time, sets the result date. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="ip_address, custom_variables, metadata, quiz_results, response_status" type="Stored only">
Submission details, when present: the respondent's IP address, custom variables, metadata, quiz results and response status. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Other response fields" type="Stored only">
Other response fields SurveyMonkey returns. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What questions does the Q3 customer satisfaction survey ask?
- What did respondents answer to "How likely are you to recommend us?" in the NPS survey?
- What did jane@example.com say in their survey response?

## How it fits into the graph

- Surveys use the Page class.
- Responses use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections SurveyMonkey creates**

- A response to its survey.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- The respondent of each response, identified by their email, when the survey collected it.

**Found in the text**

The questionnaire text and each response's questions and answers. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Surveys you did not select, and surveys past the first 100 listed.
- Responses past 25 per survey on SurveyMonkey Basic plans.
- Collectors and contacts.
- The answer choices a question offers, in the survey's searchable text.
- SurveyMonkey team sharing.
- Deletions: surveys and responses deleted in SurveyMonkey stay in HydraDB.
