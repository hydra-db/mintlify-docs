## What HydraDB is

HydraDB is a unified context layer for your AI. One brain for all your working context:

- **Memories:** Preferences, decisions, and past conversations that persist across sessions.
- **Knowledge:** Documents, app sources, and facts your AI reasons over.

Everything lives in a **database** you create. You ingest your content and connect the tools your team already uses; HydraDB builds a context graph from it and indexes it in the background.

New here? The [Quickstart](/get-started/v2/quickstart) takes you from an empty database to your first query in five minutes.

## How you get it back

One endpoint, [`POST /query`](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/query), searches knowledge, memories, or both. HydraDB searches the context graph alongside the stored content, combining semantic similarity with multi-hop graph exploration, so your agent gets the context that answers the question rather than the nearest match.

Start with the defaults. `alpha`, `mode`, `recency_bias`, and `graph_context` are yours to tune. Your context. Your opinions.

## Performance

- **Memory recall:** 90.79% on LongMemEval-S.
- **Company knowledge:** 88.7 overall on retrieval across more than half a million documents from nine fragmented enterprise systems.
- **Coding harness:** 90.73 on nlohmann/json, against 66.06 for DeepWiki and 61.28 for CodeWiki.
- **Long-term memory at scale:** 82% overall on BEAM 1M, across ten dimensions.
- **Financial AI:** 91.4% recall@10 over dense filings and enterprise data.
- **Low latency:** sub-200 ms retrieval.

The full technical breakdown of each run is in our [benchmarks](https://benchmarks.hydradb.com/).

## What else HydraDB has

- **Connectors:** 70 providers, including Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, Jira, Confluence, Linear, HubSpot, Zendesk, and Microsoft Teams, synced on a schedule. The source app's permissions come along as access control, so a query made on behalf of a user only returns what that user may see. See [Connectors](/essentials/v2/connectors) and [Access Control](/essentials/v2/access-control).
- **SDKs:** [Python and TypeScript](/api-reference/v2/sdks), with a helper that turns a query result into a prompt string.
- **Tools:** an [MCP server](/plugins/mcp) for Claude, Cursor, and other MCP clients, a [CLI](/plugins/cli), and plugins for [Claude Code](/plugins/claude-code) and [OpenClaw](/plugins/openclaw).
