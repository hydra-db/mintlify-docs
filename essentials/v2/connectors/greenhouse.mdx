Sync candidates, applications, jobs, scheduled interviews and offers from one Greenhouse account. Each record is one document. Applications link to their candidate and to each job applied for. Interviews and offers link to their application.

Greenhouse permissions are not imported, so every synced record is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Private candidates and confidential jobs are not restricted. Selecting jobs limits only jobs and applications: candidates, interviews and offers sync for the whole account. Changes to an offer after it is created may not be picked up, and records deleted in Greenhouse can stay in HydraDB.

<Note>
Greenhouse is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

<Warning>
Greenhouse permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

- Greenhouse's private flag on candidates and confidential flag on jobs are stored but do not restrict who can read them.
- An access rule on a job does not cover candidates, interviews or offers, which sync for the whole account.

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Candidates and interviews are picked up when they are updated, applications when they have new activity, and selected jobs when they are updated. Offers are picked up when they are created.
- **Edits:** An edited candidate, application, job or interview replaces its document. Changes to an older offer may not be picked up, so its status can be out of date.
- **Deletions:** Records deleted in Greenhouse can stay searchable in HydraDB.
- A new version of an offer keeps the same offer id, so it replaces the earlier version's document. Earlier versions are not kept.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

### Candidates

- **One document:** One candidate.
- **Title:** The candidate's first and last name. Otherwise `Greenhouse candidate` and its id.
- **Text:** The name, then `Company`, `Title`, `Tags` and `Emails` lines, and each past employment and education. The creation time is part of the text. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the candidate was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Resumes and other candidate attachments are not synced.

<ResponseField name="first_name, last_name" type="Searchable text">
The candidate's name, in the title and the text.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="company, job_title" type="Searchable text">
The candidate's current employer and job title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tags" type="Searchable text">
The candidate's tags.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Emails" type="Searchable text">
The candidate's email addresses, in the text only. They are not kept as a separate field.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="employments" type="Searchable text">
Each past employer and job title. Employment dates are stored but not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="educations" type="Searchable text">
Each school, degree and discipline. Education dates are stored but not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the candidate was created. It is the result date only when there is no update time.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Result date">
When the candidate was last updated. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.greenhouse_candidate_id" type="Query filter">
The Greenhouse candidate id. Finds one candidate. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (3)">

<ResponseField name="is_private" type="Stored only">
Greenhouse's private flag. It does not restrict who can read the candidate.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="application_ids" type="Stored only">

</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="last_activity_at" type="Stored only">
When the candidate last had activity.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Applications

- **One document:** One application, for a job you selected.
- **Title:** `Application:` and the names of the jobs applied for. Otherwise `Greenhouse application` and its id.

- **Date:** When the application last had activity, or when it was submitted.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="last_activity_at" type="Result date">
When the application last had activity. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="applied_at" type="Result date">
When the candidate applied. It is the result date only when there is no activity time. Not searchable.

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.greenhouse_application_id" type="Query filter">

### Offers

- **One document:** One offer. A new version with the same offer id replaces it.
- **Title:** `Greenhouse offer` and its id.
- **Text:** `Status`, `Version`, `Sent`, `Resolved` and `Starts` lines. The creation time is part of the text. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the offer was last updated, or created when there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="application_id" type="Graph connection">
Links the offer to its application. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status, version" type="Searchable text">
The offer status and version, as recorded when the offer was last read.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="sent_at, resolved_at, starts_at" type="Searchable text">
When the offer was sent and resolved, and the proposed start date.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the offer was created. It decides when the offer is synced, and it is the result date only when there is no update time.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Result date">
When the offer was last updated, as of the last time it was read. A later update does not cause it to be read again. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.greenhouse_offer_id" type="Query filter">
The Greenhouse offer id. Finds one offer. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

## How it fits into the graph

- Candidates use the Ticket class.
- Applications use the Ticket class.
- Jobs use the Ticket class.
- Interviews use the Ticket class.
- Offers use the Ticket class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Greenhouse creates**

- An application to its candidate.
- An application to each job applied for.
- An interview to its application.
- An offer to its application.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

Interviewers and organizers are not attached to an interview as people. Their names and emails are in the text only.

**Found in the text**

Candidate profiles, application answers, job details, interview details with interviewer and organizer names, and offer terms. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Jobs you did not select, and applications for them.
- EEOC self-identification records.
- Scorecards, interview feedback, candidate notes and activity feed entries.
- Resumes, photos and other files.
- Candidate phone numbers, addresses, websites and social links.
- Earlier versions of an offer.
- Deletions: records deleted in Greenhouse can stay in HydraDB.
