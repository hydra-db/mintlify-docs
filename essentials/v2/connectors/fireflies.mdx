Sync meeting transcripts from one Fireflies account. Each meeting is one document with its title, Fireflies' AI overview and the transcript, each line labelled with its speaker.

Fireflies permissions are not imported, so every synced meeting is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Audio and video are not synced. Each sync reads meetings by meeting date, so a transcript or summary that changes later is not guaranteed to be picked up. Meetings deleted in Fireflies can stay in HydraDB.

<Note>
Fireflies is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

<Warning>
Fireflies permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reads meetings from the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads meetings by meeting date, picking up meetings dated since the last sync.
- **Edits:** A change to a meeting is picked up only if the meeting's date falls inside a later sync's window.
- **Deletions:** Meetings deleted in Fireflies can stay searchable in HydraDB.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### Meetings

- **One document:** One meeting transcript. A meeting with no transcript sentences, no overview and no gist or short summary is skipped.
- **Title:** The meeting title, or `Fireflies meeting <id>` when it has none.
- **Text:** The meeting title, then the AI overview, then the transcript as one `Speaker: text` line per sentence. If there are no sentences and no overview, Fireflies' gist or short summary is used instead. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** The meeting time.
- **Link back:** The transcript in Fireflies.
- **Files:** None. Audio and video are not downloaded.

## How it fits into the graph

- Meetings use the Page class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Fireflies creates**

- No links between meetings or to other tools. The calendar event id and conference link do not link a meeting to Google Calendar or Zoom items.

**People and groups**

The connector does not attach the host, organizer or participants to a meeting as people. Speaker names are part of the transcript text.

**Found in the text**

The overview and the speaker-labelled transcript. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Audio and video recordings.
- The full attendee list Fireflies keeps. Only participant emails are kept.
- Fireflies' AI outline and shorthand bullet summaries. Only the overview, action items, keywords and, as a fallback, the gist or short summary are kept.
- Changes to older meetings whose date falls outside later syncs' windows.
- Deletions: meetings deleted in Fireflies can stay in HydraDB.
