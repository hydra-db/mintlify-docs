Sync the Loom videos whose share links you list. Each video is one document with its title, owner, description and transcript. Discord and Microsoft Teams messages that link to a listed video connect to it in the graph.

No Loom account or token is needed, and only the videos you list are synced: there is no workspace-wide listing. Loom permissions are not imported, so once a password-protected video is synced, its transcript is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Removing a link from the list does not remove the video from HydraDB, and a video deleted in Loom can stay searchable.

## Permissions

<Warning>
Loom permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Loom needs no API key or sign-in. You list the share links of the videos to sync. A password-protected video also needs its share password.

<ResponseField name="video_urls" type="string list" required>
The share or embed links of the videos to sync.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="password" type="secret string">
A share password used for every protected video that has no password of its own in `video_passwords`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="video_passwords" type="secret string">
Share passwords for individual videos, as a JSON object keyed by the 32-character video id.
</ResponseField>

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the share links">
    Copy the share link of each Loom video to sync. For a password-protected video, note its share password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Add the links as `video_urls`. For protected videos, add `password`, or `video_passwords` to give each video its own password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select videos">
    HydraDB lists one item per link and tries to read each video's title. A link it cannot read as a Loom link fails this step. A video that is missing or private is still listed, with a placeholder title, and can fail or be skipped when it syncs. Select the videos to sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads the videos you list. There is no Loom account behind it. To add a video later, add its share link.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `loom` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Syncs every listed video, whatever its age.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads every listed video again. Only videos whose content changed are updated.
- **Edits:** An edited title, description or transcript is picked up on the next sync.
- **Deletions:** Removing a link, or a video that can no longer be read, does not remove its document. A video deleted in Loom can stay searchable in HydraDB.
- If a video's transcript cannot be read, the video is skipped for that sync, so the transcript already in HydraDB is kept.
- If the password is rejected for every selected video, the sync fails.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### Videos

- **One document:** One listed Loom video with its transcript. A video with no title, owner, description or transcript is skipped.
- **Title:** The video title, or `Loom video <id>` when it has none.
- **Text:** The title, an `Owner` line, the description when it differs from the title, then the transcript. The transcript has a `Transcript` heading when other text comes before it. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the video was last updated, else when it was created.
- **Link back:** The video's share link on loom.com.
- **Files:** None. The video file is not downloaded.

<ResponseField name="owner" type="Searchable text">
The owner's display name, written as the `Owner` line. The owner is not attached to the video as a person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="description" type="Searchable text">
The owner's description of the video, written after the `Owner` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the video was last updated, written as an `Updated` line. Sets the result date.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Result date">
When the video was created. Sets the result date only when there is no update time. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.loom_video_id" type="Query filter, Graph connection">
The 32-character video id. Picks out one video. Discord and Microsoft Teams messages that link to the video connect to it by this id. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.owner_name" type="Query filter">
The owner's display name. Limits a query to one owner's videos. Not searchable under this key: the same name is searchable through the `Owner` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's videos. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `loom`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="has_transcript" type="Stored only">
Whether the transcript was included.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What does the onboarding walkthrough video say about setting up SSO?
- Which Discord messages linked to the product demo Loom?
- What did Alex explain in the Q3 roadmap video?

## How it fits into the graph

- Videos use the Page class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Loom creates**

- A Discord or Microsoft Teams message to each listed Loom video it links to, by share or embed link, up to 50 videos per message.
- The message connects to the video once the video is synced into the same collection as the message. Until then, it points to a placeholder.
- Videos have no links of their own to other items.

**People and groups**

The connector does not attach the owner to a video as a person. The owner's name is part of the video text.

**Found in the text**

The title, owner, description and transcript. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Videos whose share links you did not list.
- Video and audio files, comments, reactions and view counts.
- Removals: a link removed from the list stays in HydraDB, and a video deleted in Loom can stay.
