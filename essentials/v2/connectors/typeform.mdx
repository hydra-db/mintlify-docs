Sync forms and responses from the Typeform workspaces you select, including forms created later. Each form is one document with its numbered questions and answer options. Each completed or partial response is one document of question and answer pairs, linked to its form. Text in uploaded files can be searchable with the response.

Typeform workspace membership is not imported, so every synced form and response is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. That includes respondent emails and other answers. Forms and responses deleted in Typeform stay in HydraDB.

## Permissions

<Warning>
Typeform permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Typeform uses a personal access token. Accounts hosted in the EU also set a region.

<ResponseField name="token" type="secret string" required>
A Typeform personal access token with the scopes below.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="region" type="string">
The Typeform data center: `us`, the default, `eu` for EU accounts, or `eu_new` for EU accounts created from August 2025. An EU account without it is read from the US data center.
</ResponseField>

**Scopes the token needs**

- `forms:read`: to list the forms in each selected workspace and read their questions.
- `responses:read`: to read completed and partial responses and their answers.
- `workspaces:read`: to list workspaces and the forms in each.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a personal access token">
    In Typeform, create a personal access token with `forms:read`, `responses:read` and `workspaces:read`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the token as `token`. If the Typeform account is hosted in the EU, set `region` to `eu`, or to `eu_new` for an account created from August 2025.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select workspaces">
    HydraDB lists the workspaces the token can see. Select the ones to sync. Every form in a selected workspace syncs, including forms created later. Each workspace syncs separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads the workspaces one Typeform token can see, in one region.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `typeform` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads forms updated since the last sync, completed responses by when they were submitted, and partial responses by their `staged_at` time. Response reads overlap the previous sync by 30 minutes. A form not updated in the window is read again only when it gets new responses.
- **Edits:** An updated form replaces its document. A partial response and its completed version share one document, so they are not duplicated. Later changes to a response outside its time window are not guaranteed to come across.
- **Deletions:** Deleted forms and responses are not removed. A form or response deleted in Typeform stays searchable in HydraDB. A form that is deleted, or a workspace the token can no longer see, is skipped, and its earlier documents stay.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Typeform item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.typeform_id" type="Query filter">
The form id on a form, and the response id on a response. Targets one item. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's items. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `typeform`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type, `forms`, `responses` or `partial_responses`, shown when you inspect the item. To limit a query to completed or partial responses, use `additional_metadata.typeform_response_type`.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Forms

- **One document:** One form, with its current questions.
- **Title:** The form title.
- **Text:** The form's questions in order, numbered, with sub-questions indented and numbered 2.1 and so on. A question with choices is followed by an `Options` line. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the form was last updated, or created if there is no update time.
- **Link back:** The public form, else its address in Typeform's API.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="Questions and options" type="Searchable text">
Every question of the form and the answer options it offers.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="last_updated_at, created_at" type="Result date">
When the form was last updated and created. The update time, else the creation time, sets the result date. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="Other form fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field of the form. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Responses

- **One document:** One completed or partial response.
- **Title:** `Response to <form title>` or `Partial response to <form title>`. Without a form title, `Typeform response <id>` or `Typeform partial response <id>`.
- **Text:** The title, then a `Q:` line with the question and an `A:` line with the answer for each answer, in the form's question order. Choices show as their labels, yes or no questions as yes or no, file uploads as the file name, and payments as name and amount. Then `Hidden fields`, `Score`, `Browser`, `Platform` and `Came from` lines.
- **Date:** When the response was submitted, else its `staged_at` time, else its `landed_at` time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Files uploaded in answers. Supported files are downloaded with your token and their text is searchable with the response. If a file cannot be downloaded or read, only its name is searchable.

<ResponseField name="Answers" type="Searchable text">
Each answer, labelled by its question, so a search can match the question and the answer together.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="File uploads" type="Searchable text">
Each uploaded file's name, in its answer, and the file's text when it can be read.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="hidden" type="Searchable text">
Hidden fields passed to the form, written as the `Hidden fields` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="calculated_score" type="Searchable text">
The response's score, written as the `Score` line only for forms that define a score.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referer" type="Searchable text">
The page the respondent came from, written as the `Came from` line with only its host and path. The full address is kept for inspection.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.typeform_browser" type="Searchable text, Query filter">
The respondent's browser family, written as the `Browser` line. Limits a query to responses from one browser family.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.typeform_platform" type="Searchable text, Query filter">
The respondent's device class, written as the `Platform` line. Limits a query to responses from one device class.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.typeform_response_type" type="Query filter">
`completed` or `partial`. Includes or leaves out responses that were never submitted. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.typeform_form_id" type="Query filter">
The form id. Limits a query to the responses of one form. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="form_id" type="Graph connection">
The form the response belongs to. Links the response to its form. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="submitted_at, staged_at, landed_at" type="Result date">
Response times. The first one present, in this order, sets the result date. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="Other response fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field of the response. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What questions does the Customer Onboarding form ask, and what options does question 3 offer?
- What did respondents answer to "What is your biggest challenge?" on the Feedback form?
- What does the resume PDF uploaded in the job application response say?

## How it fits into the graph

- Forms use the Page class.
- Responses use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Typeform creates**

- A response to its form.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

No people are attached to items. A respondent's name or email in an answer is part of the text only.

**Found in the text**

Form questions and options, response answers, and the text of uploaded files. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Workspaces you did not select.
- Respondents' network ids and user agents.
- Typeform workspace membership.
- Deletions: forms and responses deleted in Typeform stay in HydraDB.
