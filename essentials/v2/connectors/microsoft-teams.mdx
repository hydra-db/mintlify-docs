Sync channel posts and replies from the Microsoft Teams teams you select, and the 1:1, group and meeting chats of the people you select. Each post, reply and chat message is one document. A reply links to its root post and a chat message to its chat, so a conversation is returned together. Text from PDF and DOCX files shared in messages is searchable when HydraDB can download and read the files.

With Enforce ACL on, a standard channel's messages can be read by the team's members, a private or shared channel's messages by that channel's members, and a chat's messages by its participants. Private and shared channels are skipped unless you turn on `include_private_channels`. Chats need the optional `Chat.Read.All` permission. Every sync fails until Microsoft approves your app for the Teams protected APIs. A message deleted in Teams becomes a hidden stub rather than being removed.

## Permissions

Turn on **Enforce ACL** for this connector to make search follow Microsoft Teams permissions. With Enforce ACL off, HydraDB does not enforce them. Only a workspace owner or writer signed in to HydraDB can turn it on. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl).

- **Who can read an item:** A standard channel's messages can be read by the team's members. A private or shared channel's messages can be read by that channel's members, including the members of every team a shared channel is shared with. A chat's messages can be read by its participants.
- **How a person is identified:** A member's email address, in lower case.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If a team, channel or chat member list is missing, empty or cannot be read in full, the message is marked with a permission warning, which you can find with `additional_metadata.rbac_warning`. A partial member list is never applied. On HydraDB Cloud, with Enforce ACL on, such an item follows the access rule you set on its resource. With no rule, it is hidden from search. Self-hosted deployments can be configured differently. Permission warnings appear in [Get Connector Status](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/get-connector-status).
- A team or channel with more than 5,000 members cannot be captured. Set an access rule on such a team.
- Do not rely on a team or channel membership change to update access right away.

You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Microsoft Teams uses a Microsoft Entra ID app registration with application permissions and a client secret. The same app can also back an Outlook connector.

<ResponseField name="tenant" type="string" required>
The Directory (tenant) ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_id" type="secret string" required>
The Application (client) ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
The client secret's Value, shown once in the Azure portal.
</ResponseField>

**Connector settings**

<ResponseField name="include_private_channels" type="boolean">
Set to `true` to also sync private and shared channels, restricted to their members. Off by default.
</ResponseField>

**Microsoft Graph application permissions to grant**

- `Team.ReadBasic.All`: lists the tenant's teams and reads each selected team's name.
- `Channel.ReadBasic.All`: lists the channels of each selected team.
- `ChannelMessage.Read.All`: reads channel posts and replies. A Teams protected API.
- `TeamMember.Read.All`: reads each team's members, who can read its standard channels.
- `ChannelMember.Read.All`: reads private and shared channel members, and the teams a channel is shared with.
- `User.ReadBasic.All`: lists people for chat selection, and resolves authors to names and addresses.
- `Chat.Read.All`: optional. Reads the chats of the people you select. A Teams protected API. Without it, no chats are offered.
- `Files.Read.All`: optional. Lets HydraDB download PDF and DOCX files shared in messages. Messages sync without it, but without the file text.

Request access to the Teams protected APIs at https://aka.ms/teamsgraph/requestaccess with your app id and tenant id. Until Microsoft approves the app, every Teams sync fails with a 403 error, even though the permissions show as granted.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register an app">
    In Microsoft Entra ID, register an app with no redirect URI and create a client secret. Copy the tenant id, the client id and the secret's Value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Add the Microsoft Graph application permissions above, not delegated ones, and grant admin consent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request protected API access">
    Request access to the Teams protected APIs with your app id and tenant id, and wait for Microsoft's approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Enter `tenant`, `client_id` and `client_secret`. Set `include_private_channels` to `true` to also sync private and shared channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select teams and people">
    HydraDB lists the tenant's teams and users. Select the teams whose channels to sync. Select a person to sync the chats that person takes part in. Chats are opted in person by person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rotate the secret">
    Rotate the client secret before it expires. An expired secret stops every sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads the teams and people you select in one Microsoft 365 tenant.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `microsoft_teams` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Each selected team and person reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads the messages modified since the last sync, per team and per selected person's chats. A channel post with a new reply is picked up again, even if the post itself is old.
- **Edits:** An edited message is read again and replaces its document. A new reply also makes its root post be read again.
- **Deletions:** A message deleted in Teams is replaced on the next sync by a stub with the text `[deleted]`. With Enforce ACL on, nobody can read the stub. The document itself is not removed.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

### Channel messages

- **One document:** One root post in a channel, or one reply.
- **Title:** The post's subject, else the first line of its text cut to about 120 characters, else `Teams message` and its id. Search matches the message text, not this title, so a subject is searchable only when it also appears in the text.
- **Text:** The message flattened from HTML, with @mention tags shown as the mentioned name. A post with no text but with attachments reads `Shared:` followed by the attachment names. The author is written as a From line and the posted time as a Sent line.
- **Date:** When the message was last modified, else when it was posted.
- **Link back:** The message's Teams web link.
- **Files:** Attachment names are kept. Text from PDF and DOCX files up to 50 MiB is searchable separately when `Files.Read.All` is granted and HydraDB can download and read the files. Images embedded in a message are not read.

<ResponseField name="parent_id" type="Graph connection">
For a reply, the root post it answers. Joins the reply to that post, so the whole conversation can be returned together. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="thread_id" type="Graph connection">
The root post's id. Groups a root post and its replies into one thread. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_id" type="Query filter">
The channel's id. Limits a query to one channel. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_name" type="Query filter">
The channel's name. Limits a query to one channel by name. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_type" type="Query filter">
Always `channel`. Separates channel messages from chat messages. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.team_id" type="Query filter">
The team's id. Limits a query to one team. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.team_name" type="Query filter">
The team's name. Limits a query to one team by name. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.channel_membership_type" type="Query filter">
`standard`, `private` or `shared`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.is_reply" type="Query filter">
`true` for replies. Keeps only root posts or only replies. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Team or channel" type="Access rule">
For a standard channel, the team's members can read the message. For a private or shared channel, the channel's members can. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="reply_count" type="Stored only">
How many replies a root post had when it was synced.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Chat messages

- **One document:** One message in a 1:1, group or meeting chat.
- **Title:** The first line of the text cut to about 120 characters, else `Teams chat message` and its id. Search matches the message text, not this title.
- **Text:** The message flattened from HTML, or `Shared:` followed by the attachment names when there is no text. The author is written as a From line and the sent time as a Sent line.
- **Date:** When the message was last modified, else when it was sent.
- **Link back:** The message's Teams web link.
- **Files:** Attachment names are kept. Text from PDF and DOCX files up to 50 MiB is searchable separately when `Files.Read.All` is granted and HydraDB can download and read the files. Images embedded in a message are not read.

<ResponseField name="thread_id" type="Graph connection">
The chat's id. Groups all messages of one chat into one thread. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="parent_id" type="Graph connection">
Also the chat's id. Keeps a chat's messages together, so earlier messages are read as context. The chat itself is not a document. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_id" type="Query filter">
The chat's id. Limits a query to one chat. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_name" type="Query filter">
The chat's topic, or up to four participant names. Limits a query to one chat by name. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_type" type="Query filter">
Always `chat`. Separates chat messages from channel messages. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.chat_type" type="Query filter">
`oneOnOne`, `group` or `meeting`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.is_reply" type="Query filter">
Always `false` for chat messages. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Chat" type="Access rule">
The chat's participants can read its messages. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="participants" type="Stored only">
The names of the chat's participants. They are not attached to the message in the graph.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What did the Platform team decide in the incidents channel about the database migration?
- What did Alex say in our 1:1 chat about the launch date?
- Show the replies to the post announcing the new on-call rotation.

## How it fits into the graph

- Channel messages use the Message class.
- Chat messages use the Message class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Microsoft Teams creates**

- A channel reply to its root post.
- Every chat message to its chat, so a chat's messages are kept together.
- A message to any Loom video it links to, which resolves when that video is synced into the same collection.
- No other links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- The author of each message.
- The people, bots and channels @mentioned in a message.
- Chat participants are kept with each chat message but not attached in the graph.

**Found in the text**

The message text. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Private and shared channels, unless `include_private_channels` is on.
- Chats of people you did not select, and every chat without `Chat.Read.All`.
- Shared file formats other than PDF and DOCX, and images embedded in a message.
- System event messages.
- Reactions, apart from their count.
