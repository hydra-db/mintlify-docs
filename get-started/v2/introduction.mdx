HydraDB is the graph-native unified context substrate for AI. It provides you with a single API for data ingestion, graph creation and interaction without the need of a query language like Cypher or Gremlin. In addition, it supports auto-parsing of documents which enables plug and play for coding agents and agentic applications. <br /><br />You ingest data on your own or pull natively from 100\+ app connectors; HydraDB automatically handles parsing and inferencing to create chunks and the context graph. 

- **Knowledge:** The central knowledge base that your AI globally reasons over. This includes uploaded documents, app sources and facts that are auto-identified post the ingestion. 
- **Memories:** User-specific preferences, identity markers and history that persist across sessions. <br /><br />**_Each unit of knowledge and memory gets its own temporal schema. This enables agents that are decision-timing and preference-evolution aware._**

# Benchmarks

At its core, HydraDB is retrieval-optimized and achieves state-of-the-art results on major public memory benchmarks, including LongMemEval-S and BEAM 1M. HydraDB is not your system of record: your primary database remains the source of truth for the data you ingest.<br /><br />Full results and the methodology are published at [research.hydradb.com](http://research.hydradb.com) 

<Info>
  Benchmark numbers are measured under controlled conditions. In production, recall varies with the quality of your data ingestion and will not automatically match benchmark figures.
