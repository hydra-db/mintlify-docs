Sync prospects, prospect accounts and opportunities from one Pardot business unit. Each prospect is one document with their contact details, company, score, grade and notes. Each account and each opportunity is one document too.

Pardot permissions are not imported, so every synced record is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. That includes prospect emails, phone numbers and notes. Stage or status changes on opportunities created before the sync window are not picked up. Records deleted in Pardot stay in HydraDB.

<Note>
Pardot is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

<Warning>
Pardot permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Pardot uses a Salesforce connected app's client id and client secret with a refresh token, together with the Pardot business unit id.

<ResponseField name="client_id" type="secret string" required>
The Salesforce connected app's client id.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
The Salesforce connected app's client secret.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="refresh_token" type="secret string" required>
A Salesforce OAuth refresh token issued through that app. HydraDB exchanges it for an access token at `login.salesforce.com`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="pardot_business_unit_id" type="string" required>
The Pardot business unit id.
</ResponseField>

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather the credentials">
    Get the client id and client secret of a Salesforce connected app, and issue a refresh token through it. Note your Pardot business unit id.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the values as `client_id`, `client_secret`, `refresh_token` and `pardot_business_unit_id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the business unit">
    HydraDB lists one `All data` resource for the business unit. Select it. Individual record types cannot be selected separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one Pardot business unit.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `pardot` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads prospects and prospect accounts updated in its window. Opportunities are read by when they were created, so only opportunities created in the window are read.
- **Edits:** An updated prospect or account replaces its document. A stage or status change on an opportunity created before the window is not picked up.
- **Deletions:** Deleted records are not removed. A prospect, account or opportunity deleted in Pardot stays searchable in HydraDB.
- When more than 200 prospects or accounts share the same update time, as after a bulk update, some of them can be skipped.
- If Pardot refuses access to one record type, that type is skipped and the others still sync. A completed sync does not mean all three types were read.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Pardot item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.pardot_id" type="Query filter">
The Pardot id of the record. Targets one record. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.pardot_object" type="Query filter">
The record type: `prospects`, `prospect_accounts` or `opportunities`. Limits a query to one type. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `pardot`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="object_type" type="Stored only">
The record type, shown when you inspect the item. To limit a query by type, use `additional_metadata.pardot_object`.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Prospects

- **One document:** One prospect.
- **Title:** The prospect's first and last name, else their email, else `Pardot prospects <id>`.
- **Text:** The title, then `Salutation`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Company`, `Job Title`, `Department`, `Industry`, `Website`, `Location`, `Territory`, `Source`, `Score` and `Grade` lines. `Location` joins city, state and country. Then the prospect's notes, or their comments when there are no notes.
- **Date:** When the prospect was last updated, or created if there is no update time.
- **Link back:** The prospect's record in Salesforce.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="email" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The prospect's email, written as the `Email` line. It identifies the prospect as a person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="first_name, last_name" type="Searchable text">
The prospect's name, written as the title and first line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="phone, company, job_title, industry, website, city, state, country, territory, source, score, grade" type="Searchable text">
Contact, company and scoring details, one labelled line each, with city, state and country joined in the `Location` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="salutation, department, notes, comments" type="Searchable text">
`Salutation` and `Department` lines, and the free-text notes at the end of the text. Notes are used before comments.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at, updated_at" type="Result date">
When the prospect was created and last updated. The update time, else the creation time, sets the result date. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="prospect_account_id" type="Stored only">
The id of the prospect account the prospect belongs to. It does not link the prospect to the account and cannot be used as a filter.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Prospect accounts

- **One document:** One prospect account.
- **Title:** The account name, or `Pardot prospect_accounts <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The title, then `Assignee`, `Created` and `Updated` lines.
- **Date:** When the account was last updated, or created if there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="name" type="Searchable text">
The account name, written as the title and first line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="assignee" type="Searchable text">
The full name, else the email, of the Pardot user assigned to the account, written as the `Assignee` line. The assignee is not attached as a person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at, updated_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the account was created and last updated, written as the `Created` and `Updated` lines. The update time, else the creation time, sets the result date.
</ResponseField>

### Opportunities

- **One document:** One opportunity.
- **Title:** The opportunity name, or `Pardot opportunities <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** Details with the opportunity's id, `Status` and `Created`, then a description with the title and `Stage`, `Status`, `Type`, `Value`, `Probability` and `Closed` lines.
- **Date:** When the opportunity was last updated, or created if there is no update time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="stage, type, value, probability, closed_at" type="Searchable text">
The opportunity's stage, type, value, probability and close date, one labelled line each.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="Searchable text">
`open`, `won` or `lost`, shown in the details and in the description.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="id" type="Searchable text">
The Pardot id, shown as the opportunity's key in the details.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the opportunity was created, shown as `Created` in the details. Sets the result date when there is no update time.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Result date">
When the opportunity was last updated. Sets the result date when present. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

**Example searches**

- What company, job title, score and grade does Pardot record for jane@acme.com?
- Which opportunities are in the Negotiation stage, and what are they worth?
- Who is assigned to the Acme Corp prospect account?

## How it fits into the graph

- Prospects use the Record class.
- Prospect accounts use the Record class.
- Opportunities use the Ticket class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Pardot creates**

- None. A prospect is not linked to its account, and opportunities are not linked to prospects or campaigns.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- Each prospect, identified by their email.
- An account's assignee is part of the text only and is not attached as a person.

**Found in the text**

Prospect details and notes, account names and assignees, and opportunity details. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Visitors, visits, visitor activities, lists, list memberships, users, email clicks and campaigns.
- Custom prospect and account fields, and other prospect details such as address, annual revenue, employees and opt-out flags.
- Stage and status changes on opportunities created before the sync window.
- Deletions: records deleted in Pardot stay in HydraDB.
