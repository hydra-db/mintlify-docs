Sync the single-send campaigns, contact lists, segments and email templates of one SendGrid account. Each record is one document: a readable summary of its fields, plus any email subject and content the record carries. SendGrid usually leaves email HTML and plain-text content out of these records, so many single sends and templates are searchable by their name, subject and settings only.

SendGrid permissions are not imported, so every synced record is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Individual contacts, sent emails, suppressions and statistics are not synced. Every sync reads all four record types in full.

## Permissions

<Warning>
SendGrid permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reads every single send, list, segment and template, whatever its age.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads all four record types in full again.
- **Edits:** A changed record replaces its document. A record with no SendGrid id is identified by a hash of its content instead, so a change to it can create a new document rather than update the old one.
- **Deletions:** Records deleted in SendGrid stay searchable in HydraDB.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### Single sends, lists, segments and templates

- **One document:** One single send, one list, one segment or one template.
- **Title:** The record's name. A record with no name is titled `Sendgrid` with its record type and id.
- **Text:** The title on the first line. Then every simple field of the record as a `Label: value` line, in alphabetical order, leaving out ids and content fields. For a template, one line per version with its name and subject. Then the email subject and the email content, with HTML converted to text, when the record includes them.
- **Date:** For a single send, its scheduled send time. For a segment or template, when it was last updated, else when it was created. A list has no date.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="Record fields" type="Searchable text">
Every field SendGrid returned is kept on the document. Simple values are searchable through their lines in the text. Nested values are kept but not searchable, apart from template version names and subjects.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.sendgrid_id" type="Query filter">
The record's SendGrid id, or a hash of its content when it has none. Picks out one record. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `sendgrid`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
Whether the record is a single send, list, segment or template.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What was the subject line of our March newsletter single send?
- Which marketing lists do we have, and how many contacts are in them?
- Which email templates exist for onboarding?

## How it fits into the graph

- Single sends, lists, segments and templates use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections SendGrid creates**

The connector creates no links. A single send is not linked to the lists, segments or template it uses.

**People and groups**

The connector does not attach records to people.

**Found in the text**

The record text. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Individual contacts and recipients.
- Individual sent emails.
- Bounces, blocks, spam reports, invalid emails and suppression lists and groups.
- Senders.
- Send and automation statistics.
- Email content that SendGrid leaves out of these records.
