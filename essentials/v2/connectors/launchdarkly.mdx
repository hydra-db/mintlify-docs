Sync feature flags, segments and environments from the LaunchDarkly projects you select, plus the account's members, teams and audit log. Each flag, segment, environment, member, team and audit entry is one document. A flag carries its configuration in every environment: on or off, prerequisites, individual targets, rules, the default rollout and its variations.

This is a record of configuration, not live flag evaluation: big-segment membership is not listed, so search cannot tell you every user who gets a variation. Project content follows LaunchDarkly's project view access only when Enforce ACL is on. Members, teams and account-wide audit entries are visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule.

## Permissions

Turn on **Enforce ACL** for this connector to make search follow LaunchDarkly permissions. With Enforce ACL off, HydraDB does not enforce them. Only a workspace owner or writer signed in to HydraDB can turn it on. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl).

- **Who can read an item:** Access follows the project. Flags, segments, environments and audit entries about one project can be read by the members whose LaunchDarkly role lets them view that project. Reader, Writer, Admin and Owner can view every project. No access views none. A member's own custom role replaces the base role, team roles add to it, and preset role statements, role attributes and permission grants are taken into account. Members, teams and audit entries about the account as a whole are not restricted.
- **How a person is identified:** A member's email address. Members who were invited but have not joined are left out.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If HydraDB cannot work out who can view a project, or an audit entry touches several projects or all of them, the connector reports a permission warning. On HydraDB Cloud, with Enforce ACL on, such an item follows the access rule you set on its resource. With no rule, it is hidden from search. Self-hosted deployments can be configured differently. Permission warnings appear in [Get Connector Status](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/get-connector-status).
- Only project view access counts. Flag-level and environment-level permission statements are not considered.
- A project with no viewers, a viewer without an email, or more than 1,000 viewers cannot be captured. Some custom role statements cannot be evaluated either.
- For permissions, the token's role must be able to read members, teams and custom roles. The built-in Reader role can.

You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

LaunchDarkly uses an access token. The Reader role is enough.

<ResponseField name="auth_token" type="secret string" required>
A LaunchDarkly access token, ideally a service token with the Reader role.
</ResponseField>

**Token role**

- `Reader`: reads projects, environments, flags, segments, members, teams and the audit log. Writer and Admin add only write access, which the connector never uses.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a service token">
    In LaunchDarkly, open Account settings, Authorization, Access tokens, Create token. Keep the role as Reader. Tick This is a service token, so the token keeps working when the person who made it is deactivated. Copy the token: it is shown once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the token as `auth_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select projects">
    HydraDB lists the projects the token can see, by name and key. Select the projects to sync. Members, teams and the audit log are read for the whole account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one LaunchDarkly account.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `launchdarkly` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Flags, segments and environments of each selected project, and the account's members and teams, are read in full, whatever their age.
- **New and changed items:** Flags, segments, environments, members and teams are read in full on every sync, and ones that did not change are skipped. The audit log is read from the newest entry already synced.
- **Edits:** A change to a flag, segment, environment, member or team is picked up on the next sync.
- **Deletions:** Items deleted in LaunchDarkly stay searchable in HydraDB. New links skip items that were deleted.
- If LaunchDarkly refuses members, teams or the audit log on the first request, they are skipped. A refusal after some records arrive fails the sync, and so does a rejected token.
- If one project is refused, the other projects still sync.
- A new LaunchDarkly organization has one empty project and no flags, so a nearly empty first sync is expected.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

### Feature flags

- **One document:** One feature flag in one project, with its configuration in every environment.
- **Title:** The flag name, else its key.
- **Text:** The name and description, then `Kind`, `Project`, `Maintainer` and `Tags` lines, and markers when the flag is temporary or archived. Then one section per environment, in order: on or off, prerequisites, individual user and context targets, rules in order, the default rollout and the off variation. Then each variation's name, value and description. Empty values are left out. A prerequisite's variation can show as a number instead of its name. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** The latest change in any environment, else when the flag was created.
- **Link back:** The flag's targeting page in LaunchDarkly.

<ResponseField name="flag_key" type="Searchable text">
The flag key. Searchable only when the flag has no name and the key is its title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="maintainer" type="Searchable text">
The maintainer's name, else their email, written as the `Maintainer` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="maintainer_id" type="Graph connection">

- **One document:** One audit log entry.
- **Title:** The entry title as plain text, else its name, else `LaunchDarkly audit` with its id.
- **Text:** The title, a `By` line with the member who made the change, the description, the short description when different, a `Comment` line, and one `Action` line per change with the action and the resource. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the change happened.
- **Link back:** The entry in LaunchDarkly.

## How it fits into the graph

- Feature flags use the Record class.
- Segments use the Record class.
- Environments use the Record class.
- Members use the Record class.
- Teams use the Record class.
- Audit log entries use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections LaunchDarkly creates**

- A flag to its maintainer member or maintainer team.
- A flag to each environment it is configured in, and to each prerequisite flag.
- A flag or segment to each segment its rules match.
- A segment to its environment.
- A member to each of their teams.
- An audit entry to the member who made it, and to the flags, environments, segments, members or teams it touched.
- Links reach only items this connector has synced, that are not deleted, into the same database and collection.
- No links to items in other tools.

**People and groups**

- Each member, identified by email.
- A flag's maintainer and an audit entry's author are linked to member records, not attached to the item as people.

**Found in the text**

Flag descriptions, audit comments and descriptions. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Flags, segments and environments of projects you did not select.
- Audit entries about a single project you did not select.
- Big-segment membership.
- Per-user targeting values outside the flag configuration.
- Custom flag properties, experiment definitions and goal ids.
- Environment and resource approval settings.
- Environment SDK keys and mobile keys, and members' MFA state and password status.
- Projects as their own documents.
- Metrics, experiments, releases and code references.
