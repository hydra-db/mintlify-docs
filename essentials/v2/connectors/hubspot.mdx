The HubSpot connector reads the CRM object types you select: deals, tickets, contacts and companies, and the notes, emails, calls, meetings and tasks logged on them. Each record or activity becomes one document.

Activities are linked to the deal, contact or company they are logged on. Records carry a list of who can read them, worked out from HubSpot owners and teams.

## Before you connect

HubSpot uses an access token from a private app, or an OAuth access token.

**Credentials**

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string" required>
A HubSpot private-app or OAuth access token. HydraDB does not refresh it, so a short-lived token must be replaced when it expires.
</ResponseField>

**Scopes for the app, all required**

- `crm.objects.contacts.read`: contacts.
- `crm.objects.companies.read`: companies.
- `crm.objects.deals.read`: deals.
- `tickets`: tickets.
- `crm.objects.notes.read`: notes.
- `crm.objects.calls.read`: calls.
- `crm.objects.emails.read`: logged emails.
- `sales-email-read`: email bodies. Without it, emails sync with their subject only.
- `crm.objects.meetings.read`: meetings.
- `crm.objects.tasks.read`: tasks.
- `crm.objects.owners.read`: record owners, used to work out who can read a record.
- `settings.users.read`: users, their teams and super-admin status, used for permissions.
- `settings.users.teams.read`: teams, used for permissions.

**Set it up**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a private app">
    In HubSpot, create a private app or an OAuth app that requests every scope above, and copy its access token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the token as `access_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select object types">
    Discover lists the CRM object types the token can read. Select the ones to sync. Notes, emails, calls, meetings and tasks are offered only when the token can also read contacts, companies, deals and tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the roles that see everything">
    When you configure the connector, list in `full_visibility_roles` the names of HubSpot roles whose members can see every record. HubSpot does not report what a role can see, so a member of such a role is otherwise not given access. Leaving the setting out keeps the current list; an empty list clears it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use provider `hubspot` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list what the credentials can reach, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

Create one connector per account, each with that account's own credentials.

## What gets synced

**What the labels mean**

- **Title:** names the item in results.
- **Searchable:** part of the text a query matches.
- **Date:** the time HydraDB uses for recency.
- **Filter:** a key a query can filter on, typed exactly as shown.
- **Link:** connects this item to another item.
- **Person or group in the graph:** a person or group the knowledge graph attaches the item to.
- **Access:** decides who can read the item.
- **Stored with results:** kept on the document, and shown when you open or inspect it. A search does not match it.
- **Not kept:** the provider has it, and HydraDB does not keep it.

## How it fits into the graph

**What each item becomes**

- Deals: a ticket, an issue, task or support ticket, with its workflow and ownership details.
- Tickets: a ticket, an issue, task or support ticket, with its workflow and ownership details.
- Contacts and companies: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.
- Notes: a comment, a note or comment that belongs to another item.
- Emails: an email, an email message, with its sender, recipients and subject.
- Calls: a message, a chat or conversation message, kept with its author and its thread.
- Meetings and tasks: a record, an item that does not fit one of the specialised kinds, searched through its text.

**Connections HubSpot creates**

- A contact to its company.
- A note, email, call, meeting or task to the deal it is logged on. With no deal, to the contact. With no contact, to the company. When several records of one type are associated, the one with the lowest id is used.
- An activity is linked to that record only if everyone who can read the activity can also read the record.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups it names**

- The record owner, as an email.
- The sender and recipient of each email.
- A contact's company name.

**What HydraDB reads from the text**

Everything in deal descriptions, ticket content, and the text of notes, emails, calls, meetings and tasks.

There is no per-connector setup for this. Every connector maps its items onto the same shared model, and HydraDB finds people, organizations, projects, products and other entities in the text the same way for every source. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Permissions

This connector can carry HubSpot permissions into search.

- **Who can read an item:** Each deal, contact and company carries HubSpot's owners and the teams that can access it, including parent teams. Its readers are the owners, every member of those teams, every super admin, and the members of the roles you list in `full_visibility_roles`. An activity can be read by everyone who can read any record it is logged on. An activity logged on nothing uses its own owner and teams.
- **How a person is identified:** A HubSpot user's or owner's email address.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If a record names owners or teams but their readers cannot be worked out, the record gets an access warning, which you can find with `additional_metadata.rbac_warning`. An access rule you set stays in force. Without one, depending on your deployment's setting, the record is either readable by everyone or hidden until its readers can be worked out. A record with no owner or team information is not restricted and gets no warning.

- Tickets do not carry HubSpot's owner and team access lists, so owners and teams do not restrict them. Set an access rule on the tickets resource if tickets must be private.
- Queues and archived owners give access to no one.
- If an activity's parent record is not synced, the activity can be readable by everyone. Sync the deals, contacts and companies your activities are logged on.
- This is HydraDB's reading of HubSpot owners, teams and roles. It is not a guarantee that HydraDB matches HubSpot's visibility in every case.

Captured permissions filter search only when **Enforce ACL** is on for the connector. Only a signed-in human workspace owner or writer can turn it on, in the dashboard connector settings. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl). You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

Database and collection names decide where content lands; collection names do not authorize access.

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Each selected object type reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads records modified since the last sync. Owners, users and teams are read in full on every sync.
- **Edits:** An edited record is read again and replaces its document.
- **Deletions:** Deleted and archived HubSpot records are not removed. They stay in HydraDB.
- If the token lacks the scope for one object type, that type can be skipped while the others sync.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` to change it.

## Not synced

- Object types you did not select.
- Email bodies, when the token lacks `sales-email-read`: those emails have their subject only.
- Products, line items, quotes, forms, marketing emails and other HubSpot objects.
- Files attached to activities.
- Custom CRM properties.
- Deletions and archived records.

## Questions this lets you answer

- What was said on the last call with this contact?
- What amounts and close dates are recorded on the synced deals?
- Which notes and emails are logged on the Acme deal?
- What did the customer write in their last email?

## Good to know

- **Stages and pipelines are ids.** Deal stages appear in the text as HubSpot's internal ids, not their display names. Pipelines are not searchable.
- **Results do not link back to HubSpot.** No HubSpot URL is stored. Use `additional_metadata.hubspot_id` to find the record.
- **Older connectors sync every object type.** A connector set up before object types could be selected keeps syncing all of them until you configure it again.
