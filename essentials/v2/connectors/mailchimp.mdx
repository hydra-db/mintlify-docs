Sync a Mailchimp account's sent email campaigns, audience lists and classic automations. Each campaign is one document with its settings, open and click rates, and the email text. Each campaign links to the audience list it was sent to.

Mailchimp permissions are not imported, so every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. Draft and scheduled campaigns, audience members, and the emails inside automations are not synced. Items deleted in Mailchimp stay in HydraDB.

## Permissions

<Warning>
Mailchimp permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Mailchimp uses an API key.

<ResponseField name="api_key" type="secret string" required>
A Mailchimp API key, complete with its data center suffix, such as `-us21`. HydraDB finds your account's Mailchimp address from that suffix.
</ResponseField>

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the connector">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the connector">
    HydraDB lists one account-wide resource, All data. Select it to sync the whole account.
  </Step>

One connector reads one Mailchimp account.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `mailchimp` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reads every sent campaign, audience list and automation in the account, whatever their age.
- **New and changed items:** Every sync reads all sent campaigns, lists and automations again. Open and click rates are refreshed on each sync.
- **Edits:** A changed campaign, list or automation replaces its document.
- **Deletions:** Deleted items are not removed. A campaign, list or automation deleted in Mailchimp stays searchable in HydraDB.
- Each sent campaign's email text is read with a separate request. If that request fails, the campaign is indexed with its settings only, and email text indexed on an earlier sync is replaced. Some failures, such as a refused request or a request that keeps timing out, can fail the whole sync.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Mailchimp item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.mailchimp_id" type="Query filter">
The Mailchimp id of the campaign, list or automation. Targets one item. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's items. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `mailchimp`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type, `campaigns`, `lists` or `automations`, shown when you inspect the item. No published filter limits a query by record type.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Campaigns

- **One document:** One sent campaign, with its email text when Mailchimp returns it.
- **Title:** The campaign's internal title, else its subject line, else `Mailchimp campaigns <id>`.
- **Text:** The title, then `Subject`, `Preview`, `From`, `List`, `Type`, `Status`, `Sent`, `Open rate` and `Click rate` lines, with the rates as percentages. Then the email text: Mailchimp's plain-text version, or the HTML converted to text when there is no plain-text version.
- **Date:** When the campaign was sent, or when it was created if there is no send time.
- **Link back:** The campaign's public archive page.
- **Files:** None. Images and files in the email are not fetched. Only the text is kept.

<ResponseField name="Email text" type="Searchable text">
The full email message, from the plain-text version or the converted HTML. The original plain-text and HTML content are not kept on the item.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="settings" type="Searchable text">
The campaign's title, subject line, preview text and sender name, written as the title and the `Subject`, `Preview` and `From` lines.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="recipients" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
The audience list the campaign was sent to. Its name is written as the `List` line, and its id links the campaign to that list.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="report_summary" type="Searchable text">
Open and click rates, written as percentages. They are a snapshot from the latest sync.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="type, status" type="Searchable text">
The campaign type and status, written as the `Type` and `Status` lines.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="send_time" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the campaign was sent, written as the `Sent` line. It sets the result date.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="create_time" type="Result date">
When the campaign was created. Sets the result date only when there is no send time. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="Other campaign fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field of the campaign, such as `emails_sent` and tracking settings. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Audience lists

- **One document:** One audience list. Its members are not synced.
- **Title:** The list name, or `Mailchimp lists <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The title, then `Permission Reminder`, `Members` and `Created` lines.
- **Date:** When the list was created.
- **Link back:** The list's public signup form, not the list in Mailchimp.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="name" type="Searchable text">
The list name, written as the title and the first line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="permission_reminder" type="Searchable text">
The list's permission reminder: its explanation of how people signed up or gave permission.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="stats.member_count" type="Searchable text">
How many members the list has, written as the `Members` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="date_created" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the list was created, written as the `Created` line. It sets the result date.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="Other list fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field of the list, such as its stats, contact details and campaign defaults. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Classic automations

- **One document:** One automation. The emails inside it are not synced.
- **Title:** The automation's title, or `Mailchimp automations <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The title, then `From`, `Status`, `Emails Sent` and `Started` lines.
- **Date:** When the automation started, or when it was created if there is no start time.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="settings" type="Searchable text">
The automation's title and sender name, written as the title and the `From` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status, emails_sent" type="Searchable text">
The automation's status and how many emails it has sent, as of the latest sync.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="start_time" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the automation started, written as the `Started` line. It sets the result date.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="create_time" type="Result date">
When the automation was created. Sets the result date only when there is no start time. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="Other automation fields" type="Stored only">
Every other field of the automation. Kept for inspection only.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What did the Black Friday campaign email say, and what was its subject line?
- What were the open and click rates of last month's newsletter?
- Which automations are running, and how many emails have they sent?

## How it fits into the graph

- Campaigns use the Record class.
- Audience lists use the Record class.
- Classic automations use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Mailchimp creates**

- A campaign to the audience list it was sent to.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

No people or groups are attached to items. Audience members are not synced.

**Found in the text**

Campaign email text, and the settings of each campaign, list and automation. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Draft, scheduled and other campaigns that were not sent.
- Audience members, segments, segment members and unsubscribes.
- Per-recipient email activity, such as who opened or clicked.
- The individual emails inside an automation.
- Images and attachments inside campaign emails.
- Deletions: items deleted in Mailchimp stay in HydraDB.
