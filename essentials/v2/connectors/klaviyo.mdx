Sync a Klaviyo account's campaigns, flows, lists, segments, email templates and subscriber profiles. Each record is one document of `Label: value` lines. Templates also carry their email content as text, and profiles carry their location and custom properties.

Klaviyo permissions are not imported, so every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule. That includes profiles, whose email, phone number and custom properties are searchable. List and segment membership is not synced. Campaign audience links describe configuration, not message delivery. Records deleted in Klaviyo stay in HydraDB.

<Note>
Klaviyo is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

<Warning>
Klaviyo permissions are not imported, so **Enforce ACL** is unavailable for this connector. Every synced item is visible to everyone who can query the collection, unless you [set an access rule on the resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).
</Warning>

API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [ACL on synced content](/essentials/v2/connectors#5-acl-native-rbac-awareness).

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default, by each record's last update time. A record not updated within that window is not synced until it changes.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads records whose update time moved past the last sync.
- **Edits:** An edited record replaces its document.
- **Deletions:** Deleted records are not removed. A record deleted in Klaviyo stays searchable in HydraDB.
- A record with no readable update time is skipped.
- Campaigns on a channel Klaviyo reports as unsupported are skipped.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Klaviyo item

<ResponseField name="Record attributes" type="Searchable text">
Every non-empty attribute with a single value, in alphabetical order as `Label: value`, such as names, statuses, personal details and dates. The `html`, `text`, `company_id`, `message` and `image` attributes are left out of this list.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at, updated" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the record was last updated. It sets the result date. When it is one of the record's attributes, it is part of the text too.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.klaviyo_id" type="Query filter">
The Klaviyo id of the record. Targets one record. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's records. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `klaviyo`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="stream_type" type="Stored only">
The record type, such as `campaigns` or `profiles`, shown when you inspect the item. No published filter limits a query by record type.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Nested details" type="Stored only">
Other nested values of the record, including list and segment membership details. Kept for inspection only, not searchable. Campaign audiences and segment definition references also create the graph connections described below.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Campaigns, flows, lists and segments

- **One document:** One campaign, flow, list or segment. Campaigns cover the email, SMS and mobile push channels.
- **Title:** The record's name, or `Klaviyo <record type> <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The title, then the record's attributes as `Label: value` lines.
- **Date:** When the record was last updated.
- **Link back:** The record's address in Klaviyo's API, not a page in the Klaviyo app.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="name" type="Searchable text">
The record's name, written as the title and as an attribute line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Campaign audiences" type="Graph connection">
Configured included list or segment audiences link through `targets`, and excluded audiences through `excludes`. These links describe audience configuration, not message delivery. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Segment definition references" type="Graph connection">
Lists or segments referenced in a segment's definition link through `related_to`. A reference in a negative rule does not assert positive membership. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

### Email templates

- **One document:** One email template.
- **Title:** The template's name, or `Klaviyo templates <id>` when there is none.
- **Text:** The title, the template's attributes as `Label: value` lines, then the email content.
- **Date:** When the template was last updated.
- **Link back:** The template's address in Klaviyo's API, not a page in the Klaviyo app.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="html, text" type="Searchable text">
The email content: the HTML converted to text, or the plain-text version when there is no HTML.
</ResponseField>

### Profiles

- **One document:** One subscriber profile.
- **Title:** The person's first and last name, else their email, else `Klaviyo profiles <id>`.
- **Text:** The title, the profile's attributes as `Label: value` lines, such as email and phone number, then a `Location` line and the custom properties.
- **Date:** When the profile was last updated.
- **Link back:** The profile's address in Klaviyo's API, not a page in the Klaviyo app.
- **Files:** None.

<ResponseField name="location" type="Searchable text">
City, region and country, written as `Location: city, region, country`. Other location details are not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="properties" type="Searchable text">
Every non-empty custom property with a single value, in order of property name, as `Label: value`. Custom properties whose value is an object or a list are not searchable.
</ResponseField>

**Example searches**

- What does the Welcome Series email template say?
- Which flows are live, and when were they last updated?
- What city, region and country are recorded for jane@example.com?

## How it fits into the graph

- Campaigns, flows, lists and segments use the Record class.
- Email templates use the Record class.
- Profiles use the Record class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Klaviyo creates**

- Campaigns link to their configured included and excluded list or segment audiences through `targets` and `excludes`, respectively; these links describe audience configuration, not message delivery.
- Segments link to lists or segments referenced in their definitions through `related_to`; a reference in a negative rule does not assert positive membership.
- Campaigns are not linked to their templates, and profiles are not linked to their lists or segments.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

No people are attached to items. A profile's name and email are part of its text only.

**Found in the text**

The attribute lines of every record, template email content, and profile locations and custom properties. List membership and other relationships are not in the text, so they cannot be found there. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Events, such as opens, clicks and orders, and metrics.
- List and segment membership.
- Campaign messages and subject lines. Template content is synced as templates.
- Klaviyo reports, such as campaign values and flow series.
- Records not updated within the first sync window, until they change.
- Deletions: records deleted in Klaviyo stay in HydraDB.
