Sync Intercom conversations from the teams you select, or from the whole workspace, with every reply in them. Intercom Tickets and published Help Center articles sync for the whole workspace. Each conversation, ticket and article is one document. Each reply is its own document, linked to its conversation. The conversation document holds only the opening message.

A conversation and a ticket can be restricted to the assigned teammate and the members of the assigned team. Search applies this only when Enforce ACL is on. Internal notes are skipped unless you turn them on. File attachments are not synced, apart from text in pasted images.

## Permissions

Turn on **Enforce ACL** for this connector to make search follow Intercom permissions. With Enforce ACL off, HydraDB does not enforce them. Only a workspace owner or writer signed in to HydraDB can turn it on. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl).

- **Who can read an item:** A conversation, its replies and a ticket can be read by the assigned teammate and the members of the assigned team. These readers take precedence over the resource's. A team resource can be read by the team's members. The All conversations resource is open to everyone who can query the collection unless you set an access rule on it. An unassigned conversation or ticket, and every article, follows its resource.
- **How a person is identified:** A teammate's email address.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If the readers of a conversation or ticket are incomplete or too many, it gets a permission warning, which you can find with `additional_metadata.rbac_warning`. Internal notes on an unassigned conversation get the warning too. On HydraDB Cloud, with Enforce ACL on, such an item follows the access rule you set on its resource. With no rule, it is hidden from search. Self-hosted deployments can be configured differently. Permission warnings appear in [Get Connector Status](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/get-connector-status).
- Intercom does not report which teammates can see all conversations. A teammate who is neither the assignee nor in the assigned team cannot read the conversation in HydraDB, even when Intercom lets them see it.
- Internal notes are synced only when `include_private_notes` is on.

You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Intercom uses an access token from a private app, or an OAuth access token.

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string" required>
An Intercom private-app or OAuth access token. An OAuth token reads only the conversations its teammate can see.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="include_private_notes" type="boolean">
Off by default. Turn it on to also sync internal teammate notes.
</ResponseField>

**Permissions for the token**

- `Read conversations`: required.
- `Read users and companies`: required.
- `Read admins`: required.
- `Read articles`: optional. Without it, articles are skipped.
- `Read tickets`: optional. Without it, or on a plan without Tickets, tickets are skipped.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a token">
    Create the token as a teammate with access to all conversations. If that teammate's access is later reduced, an OAuth token silently reads fewer conversations, so create a new token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the token as `access_token`. Turn on `include_private_notes` to also sync internal notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select teams or All conversations">
    HydraDB lists your teams and an All conversations option. Select teams to sync the conversations assigned to them, or All conversations for every conversation the token can see. All conversations takes precedence over teams. Selecting every team still leaves out unassigned conversations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one Intercom workspace.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `intercom` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads conversations, tickets and articles updated since the last sync. Every reply in a changed conversation is read again.
- **Edits:** A changed conversation is read again with all its replies, and updates their documents.
- **Deletions:** Conversations, tickets and articles deleted in Intercom can stay searchable in HydraDB.
- If Intercom refuses access to tickets or articles, they are skipped and the rest still syncs.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Intercom item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.rbac_warning" type="Query filter">
Set on a conversation, reply or ticket when its readers could not be worked out, or on an internal note in an unassigned conversation. A warning does not by itself mean the item is public. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's items. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">
Always `intercom`. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="acl" type="Access rule">
The teammates who can read a conversation, reply or ticket, worked out as described under Permissions. Articles have none of their own. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

### Conversations

- **One document:** One conversation, holding its opening message.
- **Title:** The conversation title, else the opening message's subject. Otherwise a title with the conversation id.
- **Text:** The opening message converted from HTML to text, or the title when it is empty. The status, priority, tags, creation time and conversation id are part of the text too. The title is searchable too. Search the replies for the rest of the conversation.
- **Date:** When the conversation was last updated, else when it was created.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="status" type="Searchable text">
Open, closed or snoozed, written as a `Status` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="priority" type="Searchable text">
`priority` or `not_priority`, written as a `Priority` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="labels" type="Searchable text">
The conversation's tag names, written as a `Labels` line, so a tag name in a query matches.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Conversation id" type="Searchable text">
The Intercom conversation id, written into the text as the conversation's key. Keeps later syncs on the same document.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="linked_ticket_id" type="Graph connection">
Links the conversation to the Intercom Ticket it refers to. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.intercom_open" type="Query filter">
True while the conversation is open. Keeps only open conversations. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.intercom_read" type="Query filter">
True once the conversation has been read. Finds unread conversations. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the conversation was created, written as a `Created` line. Used as the result date only when there is no update time.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Result date">
When the conversation was last updated. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (3)">

<ResponseField name="author" type="Stored only">
The opening message's author as `Name <email>`. The author is not attached to the conversation as a person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tag_ids" type="Stored only">
The ids of the conversation's tags.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="topics, topic_ids" type="Stored only">
The conversation's Fin AI topic names and their ids. They are not part of the searchable text.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Replies

- **One document:** One reply, or one internal note when `include_private_notes` is on. Parts that only record a state change, and redacted parts, are skipped.
- **Title:** `Comment by` and the author. Without an author, the first 80 characters of the text.
- **Text:** The reply converted from HTML to text, with its author, conversation id, and created and updated times. A reply that is only a pasted image is kept, and the text in the image is searchable.
- **Date:** When the reply was created.
- **Link back:** None.
- **Files:** Text in images pasted into a reply is read and searchable. Other files are not synced.

<ResponseField name="author" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The teammate or customer who wrote the reply, as `Name <email>`. The graph records the reply as written by that person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="mentions" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
Names @mentioned in an internal note. The names stay in the note text, and the graph records each as mentioned.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="parent_id" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">
The conversation the reply belongs to, written into the text as its id. Links the reply to its conversation.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.intercom_conversation_id" type="Query filter">
The id of the conversation the reply belongs to. Returns every reply in one conversation. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the reply was created, written as a `Created` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Searchable text">
When the reply was last updated, written as an `Updated` line. It is not the result date.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (1)">

<ResponseField name="part_type" type="Stored only">
Whether the part is a reply, a note or another event with text.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Tickets

- **One document:** One Intercom Ticket.
- **Title:** The ticket subject. Otherwise a title with the ticket id.
- **Text:** The ticket description converted from HTML to text, or the subject when it is empty. Then a `Reporter` line. The creation time and the ticket id are part of the text too. The subject is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the ticket was last updated, else when it was created.
- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="reporter" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The ticket's contact, written as the `Reporter` line, so tickets from one customer can be found. The graph records the ticket as reported by that person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.intercom_ticket_id" type="Searchable text, Query filter">
The Intercom ticket id. Written into the text as the ticket's key. Targets one ticket, and keeps later syncs on the same document.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the ticket was created, written as a `Created` line. Used as the result date only when the update time is missing.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Result date">
When the ticket was last updated. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="state" type="Stored only">
Open or closed. It is not part of the searchable text.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="custom_attributes" type="Stored only">
The ticket attributes your workspace defines. They are not part of the searchable text.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

### Articles

- **One document:** One published Help Center article. Drafts are skipped.
- **Title:** The article title. Otherwise a title with the article id.
- **Text:** The article body converted from HTML to text, else its summary, else its title. Then `Author` and `Updated` lines. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the article was last updated, else when it was created.
- **Link back:** The article in your Help Center.

<ResponseField name="description" type="Searchable text">
The summary under the title. Searchable when the article body is empty. Otherwise it is kept with the article.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="author" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The teammate who wrote the article, written as the `Author` line. The graph records the article as written by that person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the article was last updated, written as the `Updated` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Result date">
When the article was created. Used as the result date only when the update time is missing. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="parent_id" type="Graph connection">
The collection or section the article sits in. Places the article under it in the graph. Collections are not documents of their own. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.intercom_article_id" type="Query filter">
The Intercom article id. Targets one article, and keeps later syncs on the same document. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<Accordion title="Stored only, not searchable (2)">

<ResponseField name="collection" type="Stored only">
The Help Center collection name, or its id when the name cannot be found.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="state" type="Stored only">
Always `published`.
</ResponseField>

</Accordion>

**Example searches**

- What did customers say about the checkout bug last week?
- Which open conversations are tagged billing?
- What does our Help Center say about resetting a password?

## How it fits into the graph

- Conversations use the Ticket class.
- Replies use the Comment class.
- Tickets use the Ticket class.
- Articles use the Page class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Intercom creates**

- A reply or internal note to its conversation.
- A conversation to the Intercom Ticket it is linked to.
- An article to its collection or section.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- The author of each reply, and names @mentioned in internal notes.
- The reporter of each ticket.
- The author of each article.
- The opening message's author and the assigned teammate are not attached to a conversation as people. Assignment decides who can read it.

**Found in the text**

The opening message of each conversation, the text of every reply, ticket descriptions and article content. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Internal teammate notes, unless `include_private_notes` is on.
- Unassigned conversations, when you select teams instead of All conversations.
- Draft Help Center articles.
- Redacted conversation parts.
- Contacts, companies and segments as documents of their own. Contacts only name people.
- File attachments, apart from text in pasted images.
