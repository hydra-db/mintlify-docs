Sync emails from the Microsoft 365 mailboxes you select. Each email in each mailbox is one document, so a message received by two selected mailboxes is two documents. An email is grouped with the rest of its conversation in that mailbox. Text from supported images and PDF and DOCX attachments is searchable when HydraDB can download and read the files.

With Enforce ACL on, an email can be read only by the owner of the mailbox it came from. Recipients do not get access through someone else's mailbox. `Mail.Read` gives the app every mailbox in your tenant unless you limit it in Exchange. Drafts, Junk Email, Deleted Items and a few other folders are not synced, and permanently deleted mail stays in HydraDB.

## Permissions

Turn on **Enforce ACL** for this connector to make search follow Outlook permissions. With Enforce ACL off, HydraDB does not enforce them. Only a workspace owner or writer signed in to HydraDB can turn it on. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl).

- **Who can read an item:** Each email can be read only by the owner of the mailbox it was synced from. Recipients appear in the text and the graph, but get no access through another person's copy. To let a recipient find an email, select their mailbox too: their copy carries their own access.
- **How a person is identified:** The mailbox address, in lower case.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If HydraDB cannot resolve a mailbox owner's address, the email is marked with a permission warning, which you can find with `additional_metadata.rbac_warning`. On HydraDB Cloud, with Enforce ACL on, such an item follows the access rule you set on its resource. With no rule, it is hidden from search. Self-hosted deployments can be configured differently. Permission warnings appear in [Get Connector Status](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/get-connector-status).
- Shared and delegated mailbox access is not reflected. Only the mailbox's own address can read its emails.

You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Outlook uses a Microsoft Entra ID app registration with application permissions and a client secret. The same app can also back a Microsoft Teams connector.

<ResponseField name="tenant" type="string" required>
The Directory (tenant) ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_id" type="secret string" required>
The Application (client) ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="client_secret" type="secret string" required>
The client secret's Value.
</ResponseField>

The form also shows `include_private_channels`. It is a Microsoft Teams setting and has no effect on mail.

**Microsoft Graph application permissions to grant**

- `User.ReadBasic.All`: lists mailboxes for you to select, and resolves senders and recipients to names and addresses.
- `Mail.Read`: reads the mail of the selected mailboxes.

`Mail.Read` grants the app every mailbox in the tenant. To limit it to the mailboxes you select, create an Exchange application access policy with `New-ApplicationAccessPolicy`, naming the app id and a group, with `RestrictAccess`. A selected mailbox outside the policy fails its sync with a message that names the policy.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register an app">
    In Microsoft Entra ID, register an app and create a client secret. Copy the tenant id, the client id and the secret's Value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    Add the Microsoft Graph application permissions above, not delegated ones, and grant admin consent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit mailbox access">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Enter `tenant`, `client_id` and `client_secret`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select mailboxes">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Rotate the secret">
    Rotate the client secret before it expires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads the mailboxes you select in one Microsoft 365 tenant.

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Each selected mailbox reaches back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default.
- **New and changed items:** Each sync reads the mail in each selected mailbox whose last-modified time moved since the last sync.
- **Edits:** An email moved to another folder or recategorised is read again and replaces its document.
- **Deletions:** Mail moved to Deleted Items or Junk Email is replaced by a hidden stub only when it was received at or before the start of the time window that sync reads. With Enforce ACL on, nobody can read the stub. Other deleted mail, and mail deleted permanently, stays searchable in HydraDB.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### Emails

- **One document:** One email in one mailbox. A message received by two selected mailboxes is two documents. Drafts are skipped.
- **Title:** The subject, or `Email` and the Message-ID when there is no subject.
- **Text:** The subject, the sender, the To and CC lines and the sent time, then the email's text with HTML stripped. When there is no text body, the preview is used, else the title. Bcc recipients are not in the text.
- **Date:** When the email was received, else when it was sent, else when it was created.
- **Link back:** The message's Outlook web link.
- **Files:** Text from images, including inline images, and from PDF and DOCX attachments is searchable separately when HydraDB can download and read the files. Other attachments keep only their names.

## How it fits into the graph

- Emails use the Email class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Outlook creates**

- Every email to its conversation in its mailbox, so the conversation's emails are kept together in time order.
- No links to other tools. Copies of one email in two mailboxes stay separate documents.

**People and groups**

- The sender.
- The To, CC and Bcc recipients.
- Reply-To addresses are not attached as people.

**Found in the text**

The email text. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Mailboxes you did not select.
- Drafts, Outbox, Junk Email, Deleted Items, Conversation History and Clutter, and their subfolders.
- Attachment types other than images, PDF and DOCX. Item and reference attachments are not downloaded.
- Calendar items and contacts.
- Permanent deletions: purged mail stays in HydraDB.
