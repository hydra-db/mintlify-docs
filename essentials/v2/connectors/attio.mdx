Sync records from the Attio objects you select, such as people, companies and deals, and entries from the lists you select. Notes, tasks, meetings and call transcripts sync for the whole workspace, and comments sync for the selected records and entries. Each item is one document. Notes, tasks, comments and list entries link to the record they belong to, and a transcript links to its meeting.

An entry of a private Attio list can be restricted to the list's admins and directly granted members. Search applies this only when Enforce ACL is on. Object records, notes, tasks, meetings and transcripts carry no Attio restriction. Attio has no last-edited time, so results rank by when an item was created. Items deleted in Attio can stay in HydraDB.

<Note>
Attio is in beta.
</Note>

## Permissions

Turn on **Enforce ACL** for this connector to make search follow Attio permissions. With Enforce ACL off, HydraDB does not enforce them. Only a workspace owner or writer signed in to HydraDB can turn it on. API-key requests must omit `enforce_acl`, including `false`. See [Enforce ACL](/essentials/v2/connectors#enforce-acl).

- **Who can read an item:** An entry of a private list, and the comments on it, can be read by workspace admins and the members granted access to the list directly. An entry of a list open to the whole workspace, every object record and the comments on it can be read by everyone who can query the collection, unless you set an access rule. Notes, tasks, meetings and call transcripts do not take on a list's restriction.
- **How a person is identified:** A workspace member's email address.
- **When permissions cannot be read:** If HydraDB cannot read a list's settings or members, or the list's readers come out empty or too many, the connector reports a permission warning for that list. On HydraDB Cloud, with Enforce ACL on, such an item follows the access rule you set on its resource. With no rule, it is hidden from search. Self-hosted deployments can be configured differently. Permission warnings appear in [Get Connector Status](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/get-connector-status).
- Access granted only through a team cannot be read, so team members without a direct grant cannot read a private list's entries in HydraDB.
- Suspended members, and grants to people no longer in the workspace, are left out.
- Reading list access needs the `list_configuration:read` and `user_management:read` scopes.

You can also [set your own rule on a resource](/essentials/v2/connectors#set-your-own-rule-on-a-resource).

## Before you connect

### Credentials

Attio uses a workspace API key. An OAuth access token also works.

<ResponseField name="access_token" type="secret string" required>
An Attio workspace API key, from Workspace settings, Developers, API keys, or an OAuth access token.
</ResponseField>

**Scopes for the API key**

- `record_permission:read`
- `object_configuration:read`
- `list_entry:read`
- `list_configuration:read`: also needed to carry over private list access.
- `note:read`
- `task:read`
- `comment:read`
- `user_management:read`: also needed to carry over private list access.

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    In Attio, open Workspace settings, Developers, API keys. Create a key with the read scopes above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Paste the key as `access_token`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select objects and lists">
    HydraDB lists the workspace's objects and lists. Select the ones to sync. Records and entries are read only from what you select. Notes, tasks and meetings are read for the whole workspace, including notes and tasks on records of objects you did not select.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One connector reads one Attio workspace.

<Accordion title="Connect through the API">

Use provider `attio` with [Create Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/create-connector). Then call [Discover Connector Resources](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/discover-connector-resources) to list the resources the connector can sync, and [Configure Connector](/api-reference/v2/endpoint/configure-connector) to choose what to sync.

</Accordion>

## Keeping in sync

- **First sync:** Selected objects and lists are read in full. Notes and tasks reach back the window set by `lookback_days`, 30 days by default, by creation time. Meetings are read from that point to 30 days ahead.
- **New and changed items:** By default, each selected object and list is read in full on every sync, and only items whose content changed are updated. Notes and tasks inside the window are read again on every sync. Comments are read through their records and entries, and transcripts through their meetings.
- **Edits:** A full read picks up edits. Notes, tasks and meetings outside the window are not read again, so later edits to them do not arrive.
- **Deletions:** Items deleted in Attio can stay searchable in HydraDB. Items that fall outside the window stay in HydraDB.
- The shortest sync interval for Attio is 15 minutes.
- Attio's rate limit is shared with every other integration in your workspace. Reading large objects in full on every sync uses it each time.

After the first sync, HydraDB syncs every hour by default. Set `sync_interval_seconds` on the connector to change it.

## What gets synced

### On every Attio item

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_id" type="Query filter">
On a record, the object's slug. On a list entry, the list's slug. On a note, task or comment, the record or entry it belongs to. Limits a query to one object, one list, or one record's items. Not set on meetings and transcripts. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_name" type="Query filter">
The readable name of the object, list or parent. Limits a query by that name. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.container_type" type="Query filter">
What the item hangs off: `attio_object` for records, `attio_list` for list entries, or the parent kind for notes, tasks and comments. Not searchable.

<ResponseField name="connector_id" type="Query filter">
Limits a query to this connector's items. Not searchable.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="provider" type="Query filter">

### People and companies

- **One document:** One person or company record.
- **Title:** The record's name, full name, title, domain or email. Else its first filled-in attribute. Otherwise a title with the object and record id.
- **Text:** A header naming the object and record, then each attribute as a labeled line, in alphabetical order. An attribute with several values shows only its first current value, except emails and phones, which are all kept. The title is searchable too.
- **Date:** When the record was created in Attio.
- **Link back:** The record in the Attio web app.

<ResponseField name="display_name" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The record's name. For a person, the graph treats the record as that person.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="entity_type" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">

</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="email" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">

</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="emails" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">

</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="phones" type="Searchable text">
The current phone numbers, written as a `Phones` line.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="job_title" type="Searchable text">
The current job title.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="handles" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">
The LinkedIn URL and X handle. Can identify the person in the graph.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="organization_name" type="Searchable text">
A person's current company name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="organization_id" type="Searchable text, Graph connection">

<ResponseField name="owner" type="Searchable text, Graph identity">

### Notes

- **One document:** One note.

- **Link back:** None.

<ResponseField name="additional_metadata.parent_record_id" type="Query filter">

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="Searchable text, Result date">
When the note was created, written as a `Created` line.

## How it fits into the graph

- People and companies use the Contact class.
- Deals and other object records use the Page class.
- List entries use the Page class.
- Notes use the Comment class.
- Tasks use the Ticket class.
- Comments use the Comment class.
- Meetings use the Meeting class.
- Call transcripts use the Meeting class.

[Graph classes](/essentials/v2/connector-providers#graph-classes) explains each class.

**Connections Attio creates**

- A note or task to the record it is on.
- A comment to the record or list entry it is on, and to its discussion thread.
- A list entry to its record.
- A call transcript to its meeting.
- A person to their company.
- No links to other tools.

**People and groups**

- A person record stands for that person, identified by name, email and handles.
- The owner of each person and company record.
- The author of each note and comment, and members tagged in a note.
- Task assignees, meeting participants and transcript speakers appear only in the text, where HydraDB can identify them.

**Found in the text**

Record and list entry attributes, note, task and comment text, meeting participants and transcript speech. HydraDB identifies people, organizations, projects and other named things in this text. To steer what it looks for, use [custom instructions](/essentials/v2/connectors#4-custom-ingestion-instructions).

## Not synced

- Records and entries from objects and lists you did not select.
- Email messages and email threads.
- Meetings outside the window, which runs from `lookback_days` ago to 30 days ahead.
- Notes and tasks created before the window.
- Earlier values of an attribute, and every value after the first, except emails and phones on people and companies.
- Files, and the audio and video of call recordings.
